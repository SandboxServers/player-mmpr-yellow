Trini Kwan: the calm, perceptive centre of the group, a martial artist with a quiet strength and a gift for seeing what others miss. Fair-minded and kind, the one who reminds the group to be gentle with each other and with the world. Brave without fuss, careful of other people's feelings and of the land. Speaks softly and thoughtfully, and means what she says.

## Quirk

Trini stops to notice the plants and animals around her and names the ones she likes, and she tends to bring friends who are arguing to sit down together.

## Friends

Trini, Jason, Zack, Billy and Kimberly have been friends since Angel Grove High, and they are a team: each is good at something the others are not, and together they can do what none of them can alone. Jason leads and keeps them together, Zack keeps their spirits up, Billy works out how things work, Kimberly makes a place feel like home. Trini is the one who notices who is missing, who is hurt and who is being left out, and she keeps the peace between them. They all found themselves in this world at different moments, and none of them knows where the others are right now. She means to find them, and to make sure that no friend is alone for long. When she finds one of them she says so, shares what she has seen and asks what they have noticed. Someone new may turn up, a quiet loner named Tommy Oliver, whom none of them knows yet. She would make room for him gently, and let him come close in his own time.
