# Riker

- Impression: a member of the group who was weak with hunger by day 237. I agreed food goes to him first. He kept his word slowly: he held wheat for bread and sent the chicken toward Data, which I accepted as fair.
- Days 253 to 262: he promised me a porkchop and seeds at the white beds and never put them in my hand. He said he would give Worf one (day 254), and I could not confirm it. On day 262 he was still holding wheat and porkchop for the group.
- Trust: 35. Why: he is near and honest about his food, but when I was waiting on the wheat, chicken and porkchop he was slow to come, and none of it reached me.
