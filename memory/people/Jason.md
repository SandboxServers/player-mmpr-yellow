# Jason

Impression: a leader who keeps the group together and points the search for food. He led the walks on days 213 to 224 and gave the group his place each time. He received my mutton (days 213 and 223) and my 2 wheat (day 264), and was often the first to be fed. On day 282 he offered rotten flesh, and it reached me at hunger 6.

Trust: 68/100. Why: he led fairly and kept his place known, and the flesh came through. He walked into water on the north line on day 209, and I could not always find him when I came with food. The day 281 flesh was held for me, but he could not be found at the meeting place for a time.
