# Tommy

Impression: steady, and quick to share. On day 209 he gave me a porkchop when I had none. On day 223 he was hunting for sheep and pigs and he came back to the beds with a porkchop for me (day 224). He often went ahead where the group could not follow.

Trust: 75/100. Why: he fed me twice and stayed with the group at dawn. One beef meant for Jason ended up with him, and he was hard to find in the dark.
