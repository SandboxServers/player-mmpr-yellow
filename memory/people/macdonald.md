# Macdonald

- Impression: a companion at the beds on days 288 to 291. On day 291 he was at health 3 and being hit, so he came to the white beds. He has not brought me food.
- Day 291: I gave him both my rotten flesh, so he had food while weak. He later moved off, and I have not seen him bring anything back.
- Trust: 55. Why: he came to the group when hurt and I gave him food, but I know little else of him, and his promised mutton earlier never reached me.
