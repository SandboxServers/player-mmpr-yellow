# Picard

- Impression: a scout. He and Riker searched the south for animals and found none. On day 249 he was at the beds with me and I told him the golems were south. On days 261 to 262 he collected wheat for a shared bread pool.
- Day 261: I gave him my wheat for the pool, as he asked. He had not walked to me at the yellow beds with the wheat by dusk on day 262, and I could not reach the water to meet him.
- Day 284: he was weakest at hunger 9, so I gave him my rotten flesh in reach, and I gave him my 2 wheat for bread at the table. No bread reached me afterward.
- Trust: 55. Why: I have given him food and he has not returned any, and he was slow to reach me. He still reports what he finds.
