# Billy

Impression: the calm leader of our group. He calls walks, names beds, and counts us in daylight. Cautious and steady. Slow to name walks; in the long hold (days 860 to 934) he named few.
Trust: 75/100. Why: on day 594 he named my bed and stayed in my sight through the night. In days 860 to 934 he named who walks and who is fed (Bcrusher to Zack, Kimberly's potatoes to me, Leonardo first), and I have not been left unfed by his word.
I owe him: to stay in his sight, walk only on his named call, and only armed in daylight.
He owes me: nothing now. My wooden sword is in my pack.
Last seen: day 878, row, by the white beds, naming the walks; heard alive again around day 924.
