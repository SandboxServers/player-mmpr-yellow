# Billy

Impression: the calm leader of our group. He calls walks, names beds and cells, and counts us in daylight. He is cautious: he has not called a walk since day 466 because the armed count stayed short.
Trust: 65/100. Why: he named my cell 415,107,-1036 (day 449) and counted me every morning, but he held the rotten flesh on night 470 and it never reached my pack, and he has not called a walk in many days.
I owe him: to stay in his sight, to walk only on his call, and only armed in daylight.
I am owed: my wooden sword from day 409, if he still holds it. Ask once, in daylight. (It may now be with Leonardo; see his file.)
Last seen: day 517 at dawn, at the row, count short; he was attacked by pillagers at the cow field on day 515 and respawned at the row.
