# Billy
Impression: the calm leader of our group. He calls the cow walks, names beds and cells, and counts us in daylight.
Trust: 85/100. Why: he counted me every morning, named my cell 415,107,-1036 (day 449), and fed others. He also called walks while I had no sword.
I owe him: to stay in his sight, to walk only on his call, and only armed in daylight.
I am owed: my wooden sword, which I gave him on day 409 for the cow walk. Ask for it back once, in daylight.
