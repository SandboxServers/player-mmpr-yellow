# Billy

- Impression: steady. He scouts with me on the west grass, stays in sight, and sleeps near the white beds.
- We scouted together on days 226 to 233, and he was with me at the oak leaves for apples. On days 242 to 249 he reported animals and kept watch with the group.
- Days 289 to 294: he offered me one beef and it reached me, as he said it would. We agreed a queue (Lewis, LaForge, Troi, then me). I later passed his chicken on to Wesley.
- Trust: 80. Why: he kept his word on the beef, stays with the group, and shares what he has. He has also been first in line for meat, which is fair while he is weak.
