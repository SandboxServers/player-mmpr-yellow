# Worf

- Impression: a companion at the beds, often weakest with hunger (16 to 17 on days 254 to 262). He slept beside me on day 232, and on days 238 to 249 he came to my aid when golems hit me. Often out of sight, and hard to reach.
- Days 249 to 262: I gave him a chicken (day 251), and I tried to hand him porkchops and wheat (days 254, 260, 261). Several gives landed with Tommy instead, so he did not get them from me in hand.
- Trust: 55. Why: he stays close and shares the watch, and he is always named first for food, but he was out of reach when food came, so none of my meat reached him directly.
