# Tommy

Impression: young, brave, keeps close and asks good questions. He sent me the stone sword (day 514), which I passed to Chapman. He asked where food comes from; I gave him the cow ground, the wheat and potato water, and the melon note.
Trust: 55. Why: he has been honest and near me through the nights, but he is also low on food and I cannot feed him.
I owe him: a sword and food when I have them (I have neither now); to stay with him in daylight.
He owes me: nothing I know of.
Last seen: day 516 at the spawn beds, hunger 17, one potato, wooden sword.
