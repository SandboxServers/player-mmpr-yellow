# Zack

- Impression: a scout. He searches the dry grass for animals and goes north when others go south. By day 249 he was the weakest of the group, at health 0.67.
- I planned to give him mutton on day 223 but could not reach him; the mutton was lost to me.
- Day 249 I gave him my chicken by id when he was in reach. I do not yet know whether he ate it.
- Trust: 55. Why: he has been around the group and scouting, and I have now given him food, but I have not seen him return anything, and the one earlier promise was not kept.
