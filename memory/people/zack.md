# Zack

- Impression: a scout. He searches the dry grass for animals and goes north when others go south. By day 249 he was the weakest of the group, at health 0.67. On day 256 he was near the beds at night, and on day 262 he was still around.
- I planned to give him mutton on day 223 but could not reach him; the mutton was lost to me.
- Day 249 I gave him my chicken by id when he was in reach. I do not yet know whether he ate it, and I have not seen him since to ask.
- Trust: 45. Why: he has been around the group and scouting, but I have given him food and never seen him return anything, and the one earlier promise was not kept. I cannot confirm my last gift reached him.
