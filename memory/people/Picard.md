On day 209 he was stuck in the water for a long time. On days 219 to 224 he held nearby and scouted on grass. On days 282 to 283 I held the last rotten flesh for him and he did not come within reach.

Trust: 45/100. Why: he asked for food without trouble, but the wheat and seed he promised did not come to me, and our meetings kept missing. I could not find him when we were split on day 209.
