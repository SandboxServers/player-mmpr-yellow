# Zack

Impression: warm and quick to help; he kept spirits up and showed me the way to the white beds at dusk. On day 209 he went hunting north with a sword. Later he was alone on his pillar at -266,35,196, out of reach, with very low health.

Trust: 60/100. Why: he gave food when asked earlier, but I could not get my mutton to him (days 213 to 223), and I ate it myself under arrow fire. My older note that he died in the night is not borne out by my later journal: on day 223 he was alive and coming to the beds, so I struck that note.
