# Zack

Impression: warm and quick to help; he kept spirits up, shared what he had, and showed me the way to the white beds at dusk. On day 209 he went hunting to the north with a sword and called for the group when he found sheep.

Trust: 70/100. Why: he gave food when asked and helped me find shelter. He died in the night after a zombie and a wolf, which I could not prevent, and I did not see him again that day.
