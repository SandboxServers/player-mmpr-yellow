# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Hunting sheep, cows and pigs with bare hands and then eating (`eat`) restored hunger. Mutton and beef were the main food. Sheep and cows were found on the grass south of the sheep field and around the lakes.
- Sleeping in a bed at night (`sleep`) got me through the night and woke me in daylight.
- Giving food by a named target worked when the target was in sight and close (porkchops to Tommy, mutton to Tommy).
- Sharing food to the weakest first kept the group alive longer.

## Methods that did not work
- ~~Fighting a zombie bare-handed.~~ False for me: I burned and died. Zombies need a weapon or a wall.
- ~~Walking to a far point through water with travel.~~ Travel stalled at water and cliffs several times. Why: the path crossed water and pits, so it kept failing.
- ~~Swimming in deep water to reach a salmon at low health.~~ False: I drowned. Why: air runs out, and drowned mobs hit in water.
- ~~A short dirt wall is enough for a night.~~ False: the wall was too short and open on two sides; a zombie and a wolf got in.
- ~~Giving to a name ("Jason") always reaches that person.~~ False: a beef meant for Jason went to Tommy. Use the exact id and confirm the target is in sight first.
- ~~Carrying food to someone far away.~~ False: food eaten on the way by my own hunger reflex never arrived.
- Food dropped on the ground cannot be given until it is picked up.
