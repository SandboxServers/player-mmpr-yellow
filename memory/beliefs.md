# Beliefs

What I hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Eating (`eat`) when hungry restored hunger. Mutton, beef and porkchop held. Eating at 3 health under arrow fire kept me alive (day 223).
- Sleeping in a bed at night works when I stand beside a free bed square and sleep (white or yellow beds, days 218, 219, 223).
- Giving food by exact id works when the person is in sight and close (porkchop to Kimberly by id, day 224).
- Sheep at -184,86,230 gave mutton (day 223). A pig was killed on dry grass near -171,74,201 (day 224).
- Sharing food to the weakest first kept the group alive longer.
- Walking with the group in sight, on the known dry route east (-262,66,232, -210,70,228, -189,80,223), kept us together.
- Calling out the position before moving, so others know where to find me.
- Placing a plank at my feet and trying sleep again let me sleep when monsters were near (day 219).

## Methods that did not work
- ~~Fighting a zombie bare-handed.~~ False for me: I burned and died. Zombies need a weapon or a wall.
- ~~Walking to a far point through water with travel.~~ False as a rule: travel stalled at water and cliffs on days 209 and 213. Why: the path crossed water and drops.
- ~~Swimming in deep water to reach a salmon at low health.~~ False: I drowned. Why: air runs out, and drowned mobs hit in water.
- ~~Walking alone to a lake shore or a sheep by the water.~~ False: I drowned at -263,61,207 on day 209. Why: I went without the group.
- ~~A short dirt wall is enough for a night.~~ False: a wall of planks did not place (day 219), and an open wall let a zombie and a wolf in (day 209). Why: I had too few planks, and the build failed.
- ~~Giving to a name ("Jason") always reaches that person.~~ False: a beef meant for Jason went to Tommy. Use the exact id and confirm the target is in sight.
- ~~Holding food for someone far away.~~ False: I held mutton for Zack for two days, could not reach him, and ate it myself under arrow fire (day 223). Why: the route was never walked.
- ~~Walking unarmed toward skeletons or the west shore.~~ False: a skeleton shot me to death on day 209, and I shot to 3 health again on day 223.
- ~~Walking alone toward the east pig carrier route.~~ False: the carrier was killed by an unseen attacker near -241,268 (day 224). Why: no one walked with him.
- ~~Hunting a cow at the spot where it was listed.~~ False: cows were gone on day 209, and none were found near the listed spots on days 219 to 224. Why: animals move.
- A bed square that is occupied refuses me silently (day 214). Try another bed square.
- Food dropped on the ground cannot be given until it is picked up.
