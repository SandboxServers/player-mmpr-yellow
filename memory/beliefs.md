# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Eating (`eat`) after a kill restored hunger. Mutton, beef and porkchop were the food that held. Sheep and cows were found on the grass south of the sheep field, and around the lakes, but not always where they were listed.
- Sleeping in a white bed at night (`sleep`) got me through the night and woke me in daylight.
- Giving food by a named target worked when the target was in sight and close (porkchops to Tommy, mutton to Tommy).
- Sharing food to the weakest first kept the group alive longer.
- Walking with the group in sight, along dry grass, and holding until everyone was close, kept us together at least once (day 209, the walk south to the cow spot).
- Calling out the position before moving, so others know where to find me.

## Methods that did not work
- ~~Fighting a zombie bare-handed.~~ False for me: I burned and died. Zombies need a weapon or a wall.
- ~~Walking to a far point through water with travel.~~ False as a rule: travel stalled at water and cliffs again on day 209. Why: the path crossed water and drops, so it kept failing.
- ~~Swimming in deep water to reach a salmon at low health.~~ False: I drowned. Why: air runs out, and drowned mobs hit in water.
- ~~Walking alone to a lake shore or a sheep by the water.~~ False: I drowned at -263,61,207 on day 209. Why: the lake was in the direct line, and I went without the group.
- ~~A short dirt wall is enough for a night.~~ False: the wall was too short and open on two sides; a zombie and a wolf got in.
- ~~Giving to a name ("Jason") always reaches that person.~~ False: a beef meant for Jason went to Tommy. Use the exact id and confirm the target is in sight first.
- ~~Carrying food to someone far away.~~ False: food eaten on the way by my own hunger reflex never arrived.
- ~~Walking unarmed toward skeletons or the west shore.~~ False: a skeleton shot me to death on day 209. Why: I had no weapon, and I went into its line of fire.
- Food dropped on the ground cannot be given until it is picked up.
- Hunting a cow "by hand" at the spot where it was listed: the cows were gone on day 209. Why: animals move; a listed position is only a hint.

## People and places
- The white and yellow beds near -270,67,336 and -272,68,364 are where the group sleeps.
- The north-west lake at -275,60,182 is deep water with drowned mobs. Stay out of it.
- The lake west and south-west of the beds (around -265 to -340, 60 to 265) is deep water with drowned and skeletons on its shores. Do not cross it. Dry routes near the beds are the only ones I trust.
- A creeper and a wolf roam the sheep field and the lake edges at night.
