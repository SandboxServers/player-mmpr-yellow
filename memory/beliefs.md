# Beliefs

- Chickens near 421,107,-989 and 469,107,-1026 have been found empty or nearly so; they come and go.
- The beds at 443,107,-1022 and 443,107,-1023 are good for sleeping. Using a bed from the side can fail; stepping onto the bed square and sleeping works.
- Food chests: 433,107,-1051 (chicken) and 433,107,-1023 (melon).
- Speaking to a friend by name with their position works best when they are in sight. Sight is needed for giving.
- Health does not come back while hunger is low, so food comes before anything else.
