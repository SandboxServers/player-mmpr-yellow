# Beliefs

What I hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Eating (`eat`) when hungry restored hunger. Mutton, beef, porkchop and chicken held. Eating at 3 health under arrow fire kept me alive (day 223).
- Sleeping in a bed works when I stand beside a free bed square and sleep, and no monster is close (white or yellow beds, days 218, 219, 223, 226, 228).
- Giving food by exact id works when the person is in sight and close (porkchop to Kimberly by id, day 224; apple to Kimberly, days 226 and 231; chicken to Zack by id, day 249).
- Sheep at -184,86,230 gave mutton (day 223). A pig was killed on dry grass near -171,74,201 (day 224).
- Sharing food to the weakest first kept the group alive longer.
- Giving a porkchop by exact id to Jason, when he was in reach, worked (days 254 and 262). Giving by id to Worf did not always land: one porkchop went to Tommy instead (days 254, 260).
- Going to a person and standing within arm's reach before the give is the only way the give reached them (day 254, Clark's rotten flesh).
- Walking with the group in sight, on the known dry route east (-262,66,232, -210,70,228, -189,80,223), kept us together.
- Calling out the position before moving, so others know where to find me.
- Mining oak leaves gave one apple once (day 226, at -283,66,324). Most leaf breaks after that gave only saplings (days 228 to 236).
- Breaking grass by the beds gave seeds (day 236), which Wesley wanted for wheat.
- A wooden hoe made from planks and sticks at a placed crafting table tilled grass (day 238).

## Methods that did not work
- ~~Fighting a zombie bare-handed.~~ False for me: I burned and died. Zombies need a weapon or a wall.
- ~~Walking to a far point through water with travel.~~ False as a rule: travel stalled at water and cliffs on days 209 and 213. Why: the path crossed water and drops.
- ~~Swimming in deep water to reach a salmon at low health.~~ False: I drowned on day 209, and on day 233 I was in the west water after salmon and got out only by climbing back to the bank. Why: air runs out, and drowned mobs hit in water.
- ~~Walking alone to a lake shore or a sheep by the water.~~ False: I drowned at -263,61,207 on day 209. Why: I went without the group.
- ~~A short dirt wall is enough for a night.~~ False: a wall of planks did not place (day 219), and an open wall let a zombie and a wolf in (day 209). Why: I had too few planks, and the build failed.
- ~~Placing a plank at my feet and trying sleep again lets me sleep when monsters are near.~~ False in days 232 to 249: sleep was refused again and again with golems, phantoms or a creeper 4 to 20 blocks away. It worked only when they were out of range. Why: the plank is not a wall.
- ~~Giving to a name ("Jason") always reaches that person.~~ False: a beef meant for Jason went to Tommy. Use the exact id and confirm the target is in sight.
- ~~Holding food for someone far away.~~ False: I held mutton for Zack for two days, could not reach him, and ate it myself under arrow fire (day 223). Why: the route was never walked.
- ~~Walking unarmed toward skeletons or the west shore.~~ False: a skeleton shot me to death on day 209, and I shot to 3 health again on day 223.
- ~~Walking alone toward the east pig carrier route.~~ False: the carrier was killed by an unseen attacker near -241,268 (day 224). Why: no one walked with him.
- ~~Hunting a cow at the spot where it was listed.~~ False: cows were gone on day 209, and none were found near the listed spots on days 219 to 224. Why: animals move.
- ~~Scouting the dry grass or west grass for animals finds food.~~ False on days 226 to 249: no animals were found on the dry grass or west grass. Why: the golems and the group had cleared the area, and animal positions went stale.
- ~~Waiting for a bed to free up when golems are near.~~ False: occupied beds refused sleep (days 214, 233, 236, 249). Why: the group crowds the beds at dusk, so I should go to a bed square early.
- ~~Eating raw wheat or rotten flesh to ease hunger.~~ False: "eat" refused raw wheat (day 238), and rotten flesh did not register (day 248). Why: the food was not edible in that form, or eating was refused.
- ~~Eating at hunger above 6.~~ False as far as I can tell: "eat" was refused at hunger 10 and 11 (days 245 to 249). Why: unknown; I should wait until hunger is 6 or lower before I count on eating.
- ~~Food promised by someone on the ground will reach me.~~ False: a chicken dropped on the ground by Jason never entered my pack (day 248). Why: items on the ground cannot be given or taken until picked up by the one who needs them.
- ~~Riker's porkchop, promised on days 253 to 254, would reach me.~~ False: it never came into my hand, and I went without. Why: promised food by someone who cannot reach me does not arrive.
- ~~Mutton or wheat sent to me from far away would reach me by dusk.~~ False for days 256 to 262: I could not walk to Picard at the water, and dusk stopped the trip. Why: the route was across water.
- A bed square that is occupied refuses me silently (day 214). Try another bed square, and try early at dusk.
- Food dropped on the ground cannot be given until it is picked up.
- Planting a seed on tilled dirt at -268,65,326 did not stick through days 238 to 249. Why: unclear; the seed stayed in my pack, and a second attempt at first light did not confirm it in the ground.
