# Beliefs

## Worked
- Standing in Billy's sight with armed friends and food nearby kept me alive through most nights (days 460-517, health held at 20 while I stayed in reach).
- Walking only armed, in daylight, on Billy's call, with four in sight. (Billy has not called a walk since day 466: the armed count stayed short.)
- Sleep in the cell Billy names. The named cell is 415,107,-1036 (day 449). Sleep worked at 413,107,-1041 on day 406, when that bed was free.
- Eating: health rose from 7 to 12 at hunger 17 on days 411 and 412, so health does recover while hunger is middling; keep food in the pack anyway.
- Taking a sword into hand: holding it in the pack and clicking plain open ground worked once for Tommy (day 517 note); Zack's method (stand still on plain ground, not a bed) is the one I trust.
- A give to someone in sight goes to their pack at once. It goes into their hand only if they take it. Stone sword to Chapman worked (day 515). Give to Leonardo failed once (items on the ground near him).

## Failed or false
- ~~Health does not regenerate while hunger is low.~~ False: see above. Health can rise at hunger 17.
- Walking unarmed: a pillager shot me on the cow walk on day 414. Unarmed walks have killed me.
- Phantoms strike at dusk and night, even at the bed row with the group in reach. I died to them at the row on days 403, 417, 432, 448, and 470, and again at night near 412-413,-1039 after that. Stay in sight; never walk out at dusk; never stand unarmed on open ground at night.
- Dropped things are rarely recovered. The pile at 480,104,-1136 was gone. The sword I dropped at the death spot (413,108,-1039) on night 470-471 did not come back to my pack. The sword and flesh I left at 388,-1043 on day 448 were gone.
- Sleeping in beds other than the named cell has been refused or taken (401 beds, 387 beds, 413,107,-1041 later).
- Cow ground at 480,-1137 is dangerous at night: phantoms killed me there. Do not walk there after dusk.
- Cows at 364,-1010 were empty on the last several walks.
- Cows were once in the open grass near 459,107,-951; one cow gave 2 beef.
- ~~Tell next: Kimberly and Jason about the day-406 bed sleep.~~ Stale; dropped.
- A give to Leonardo on day 514 went to Chapman first; his wooden sword later reached Leonardo's pack, not his hand. Do not count a give as done until the recipient says it is in hand.
