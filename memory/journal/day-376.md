Day 376. Woke at the west white bed, health 15.3, hunger 17. Said good morning to Riker and Beverly and asked where Jason, Kimberly and Billy are; nobody has seen them since waking. Beverly, Zack, Riker, MacDonald, Clark and Johnny (Chapman) all at the beds. Riker has no food to spare; ripe wheat and potatoes are by the water at 462-463,107,-1073 to -1087. Billy's cow walk still not called; Zack and Riker wait for it at first light. At dusk Zack and Chapman said goodnight; I wrote the handoff and lay down to sleep beside the west bed, group in sight.
