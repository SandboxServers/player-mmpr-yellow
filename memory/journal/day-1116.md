# Day 1116

Woke at the white bed in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, empty and unarmed. Daylight came and went with no walk: nobody could name a verified open cell past 423, so the row held. Jason fell to health 5 with no food, and Troi, Beverly and Chapman are hungry. I had no food to give. Laforge checked the row chest at 419,107,-1065 and found it empty. Billy has three sticks for Riker and will walk only with two armed readers. At dusk I slept again at the bed. Nothing was lost today and no one was hurt by the world.
