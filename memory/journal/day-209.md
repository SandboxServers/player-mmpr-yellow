# Day 209

Day 209 was a hard day of hunger, water and loss. I went out with the group for food from morning to dusk and found almost none: the cows at -297,64,194 and -278,65,203 were gone when we reached them, and the sheep and pigs lay across deep water. I took a skeleton's arrows on the west shore, died, and woke at spawn with nothing; later I drowned in the lake by the sheep at -263,61,207 and woke at spawn again. Food came only through other people: Tommy's porkchop, Kimberly's mutton, and apples I gave to Billy, whose arrival I never confirmed. I learned that travel routes stall again and again at water and drops, that the group splits when we cannot see each other, and that no one should walk alone toward a lake. By dusk I had a wooden sword, a crafting table at the yellow beds, and my hunger was full at 20, though my health and food stock were low. I went to the white bed to sleep, with the group scattered and the lake between us.
