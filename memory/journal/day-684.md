# Day 684

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day, as asked. No walk was named. Hunger stayed at 17 and I hold no food. Lewis, Zack, Billy and Picard all said they had none to spare, or would share only if called. Zack offered one potato if I stand in his sight, but he would not walk to me without Billy's call. Billy's rule: stay at the bed until hunger is 10, then tell him and we find food. Health stayed steady at 17. Night came and I slept at my bed beside the row.
