Day 955. Woke at my white bed in Billy's row, 444,107,-1051, health 11, hunger 17, holding 2 potatoes. Said good morning to the row. Troi, Riker, Beverly and William all held at their beds waiting for Billy's named daylight walk. Leonardo, at health 1, is somewhere out of sight; Troi says he is still calling. I told him where I am and that I hold his potatoes, and asked him to say where he is. No one came into sight, so the potatoes stayed with me. No walk was named, so I did not leave the row. Went to sleep at dusk at my bed.
