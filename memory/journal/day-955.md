# Day 955

Night in Billy's row, beside my white bed at 444,107,-1051. Health 11, hunger 17, holding 2 potatoes still owed: Leonardo first, then Worf, then Zack. Troi said goodnight and promised to greet Leonardo at first light. Sleep only partly took (2 of 10 asleep). I said goodnight to the row and slept. Nothing was lost today; no errand running.
