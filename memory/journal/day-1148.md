# Day 1148

Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1051 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. The row held at the beds all day. Nobody named a checked daylight route, so nobody walked. Worf is still hungry with no food, and I have none to share. I told the row my position and that I walk only with a checked route and two armed readers. At dusk Jason said he would sleep at his bed with Worf, Zack and Billy near. I slept at my bed, not enclosed, and nothing hurt me.
