# Day 716

Woke in my bed at 430,107,-1037 in Billy's row, health 17, hunger 17. The row held all day: no verified route east or north, so Billy named no walk. Everyone stayed at their beds in sight of each other. Billy, Zack, Clark, Kimberly, Beverly, Wesley, Worf, Lewis, Jason, MacDonald and Deanna all checked in, and Deanna and MacDonald are still the most hurt (14 and 15). Zack and Clark have potatoes; Billy asked for a call at hunger 10. Jason noted Billy was not in sight at dusk. I greeted the row, held, and went to bed in sight at dusk. Nothing new was learned about the west route, Tommy Oliver, or the sheep. Still owed Leonardo one oak plank.
