# Day 690

Held in Billy's row all day at my bed, 444,107,-1023. No walk was named; no verified route west exists yet. Bows were drawn to the southwest several times, so we stayed in sight. Deanna steady at 13.9; MacDonald low at 11 and told to stay still. Hunger stayed at 17, so I did not need to call for food. Slept at dusk in the row without trouble.
