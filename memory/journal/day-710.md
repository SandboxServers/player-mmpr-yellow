# Day 710

Woke in the row at first light. Greeted everyone; Billy, Kimberly, Zack, Wesley, Beverly, Lewis, Deanna and MacDonald all held at their beds with no walk named, since no verified west route exists yet. Zack and Clark offered food; I declined because hunger was 17, and I will call at 10. Night came, and I slept in the row near a bed in Billy's sight, nine of ten asleep. Nothing new was found, and nobody went out.
