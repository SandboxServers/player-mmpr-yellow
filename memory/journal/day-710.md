# Day 710

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day, at 429,107,-1037. No verified west route and no walk named by Billy, so no one went out. Health held at 17, hunger at 17. Greeted the row in the morning. MacDonald is the most hurt (health 11), and I told him to stay close and keep his potatoes near. Deanna is at 13.9. Kimberly holds the potato for hunger 10. Beverly's bed refused her sleep, which Billy has no fix for. Went to bed at dusk in the row, in Billy's sight.
