# Day 740

Woke at dusk-to-night in Billy's row beside Chapman, health 17, hunger 17. Nothing new came in: no walk named, no food, no plank. MacDonald sleeping near 429,107,-1051, in Billy's sight; he reported health 15, hunger 16. I said goodnight to the row and slept in my bed at 443,107,-1064. Still owe Leonardo one oak plank. Tommy Oliver and the cows not seen today. Next: hunger 10, call Billy, Zack, Clark or Kimberly for food.
