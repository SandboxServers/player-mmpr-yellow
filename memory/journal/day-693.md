# Day 693

Held in the row all day in Billy's sight. No walk was named, and bows were heard drawn to the southwest several times, so nobody went out. My first sleep attempt on the night of day 692 was refused because it was not yet night; I walked back to the row and waited. At the next dusk I slept in my bed at 443,107,-1022 with the row around me. Health 17 and hunger 17 all day, steady. MacDonald's health was low (11) and I asked after him; Deanna is steady at 14. Kimberly and Zack will call at hunger 10, and Billy will feed anyone who calls. Nothing new was found, and the west route is still unverified.
