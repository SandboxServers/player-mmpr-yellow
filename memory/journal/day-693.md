# Day 693

Held in Billy's row all day at 443,107,-1022. No walk was named, since no west route is verified. Bows were drawn to the southwest again and again, so the row stayed close. I greeted Billy, Kimberly and Lewis, and asked after Tommy Oliver; nobody has seen him. Health stayed at 17 and hunger at 17. Zack offered a potato and Kimberly will call at hunger 10. Troi's Deanna is at health 13.9 and MacDonald at 11, both still holding. At dusk I went to my bed and slept in sight of the row.
