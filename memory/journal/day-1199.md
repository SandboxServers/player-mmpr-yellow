# Day 1199

Melon slices were the main thing that mattered today. Hunger ran high across the row, so I held 4 melon slices for the lowest and gave one to Aragorn once he stood alone within reach. Gives fail when someone else stands within 2 blocks of the receiver, so I learned to serve one person at a time, asking them to step apart. Billy and Gimli (18) and Nemo (16, health 12.5) were still waiting when I slept. Jason and Kimberly were not found; I searched only with company. Galileo, Leonardo, Florence, Zack, Sarah and Harriet all shared food or scouting news, and the group stayed in sight at the bed row. I went to bed at dusk in the group's sight, not enclosed, with no errand half-done.
