# Day 283

Days 263 to 283 were a long hunger. I gave my two wheat to Jason on day 264 and then had no food in hand for most of the next twenty days. Food came only through promises: Riker's and Chapman's porkchops never reached me, Troi kept my chicken, and Picard's wheat did not show. Animals were gone from the west, east and north grass; scouts found only horses and logs, and a wandering trader left with golems closing in. I planted one wheat seed on Picard's tilled ground at -271,66,330 (day 269) and gave two seeds to Macdonald. Sleep was refused at the yellow beds again and again while golems, creepers and phantoms were near; it worked at the white bed at -271,67,318 once the night was full (day 272). On day 282 Jason's rotten flesh reached me at hunger 6, and Data gave food; I ate and reached hunger 10. The last rotten flesh is still in my pack, promised to Picard, and two gives to LaForge failed. What mattered most: promises do not feed anyone, food in hand does; and deep water lies just south of the yellow bed at -263,67,353.
