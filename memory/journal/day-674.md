# Day 674

Held all day at my white bed in Billy's row at 430,107,-1051, sword in hand. Billy still had no verified west route, so no walk was named. I greeted Picard, Tommy and the row, and listened. Health 17 and hunger 17 stayed steady; I was not fed because nobody was at hunger 10 or below. Dusk came and I slept beside my bed in sight of the row. Tommy is with us at 429,107,-1065; MacDonald is still thin at health 11.
