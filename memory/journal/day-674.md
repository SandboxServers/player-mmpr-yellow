# Day 674

Woke at my bed in Billy's row with health 17 and hunger 17. Billy named no walk all day: no west route is verified yet, so everyone in the row, Trini included, held at their beds in his sight. Tommy, Riker, Chapman, Worf, Jason, Picard, Laforge and Lewis all reported holding. MacDonald is still at health 11, and Tommy and MacDonald have no food. Billy said he will feed anyone at hunger 10 or below first. I greeted the row and held. Nothing was dug, no one was hurt, and I went to sleep at dusk beside my white bed at 443,107,-1050 in Billy's sight.
