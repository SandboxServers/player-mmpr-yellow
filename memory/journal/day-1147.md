# Day 1147

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day and night. No walk, no route named. Lewis, Jason, Worf, Riker, Wesley, Laforge, John, Kimberly and Zack all held at their beds and said so. Jason is at health 5 with no food; Worf and Zack are first in line for any food. I said my position to Kimberly and to Lewis. Slept at the bed with health 20 and hunger 20. Nothing new found; still no checked daylight cell past 423.
