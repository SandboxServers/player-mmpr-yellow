# Day 714

Held in my bed in the row all day in Billy's sight. Billy kept the row at beds: no verified west route, so no walk. Greeted the row at dawn. A pillager with a crossbow was seen about 22 blocks southeast near 454,107,-1002; Kimberly watched it and Billy told everyone to stay out of the open. MacDonald ate his second potato. Health 17, hunger 17 at sleep. Slept in my bed at dusk.
