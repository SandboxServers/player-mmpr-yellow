# Day 714

Slept in my bed at 430,107,-1037 in the row, in Billy's sight. Health held at 17 and hunger at 17 all day; no food on me. The row held without a walk, since Billy named none. Zack offered Troi a potato, Clark offered potatoes at hunger 10, and MacDonald ate a potato on Billy's word. Nothing new was found; the west route is still unverified and Tommy Oliver has not been seen. The main thing is that the row is steady and I am sheltered.
