# Day 714

Dusk in the row. I stayed in my bed at 430,107,-1037 in Billy's sight, health 17, hunger 17. Jason, Clark and Kimberly all held their places and said goodnight; no walk was named, and no one was hurt. Nothing new was found: no west route, no Tommy Oliver, no sheep. I slept in the bed. Still owed: one oak plank for Leonardo, and food to be called for at hunger 10.
