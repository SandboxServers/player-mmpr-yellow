# Day 798

Held in the row at 430,107,-1023 through the morning on Billy's word. Bows kept drawing to the south all day, and nobody left the beds. Billy asked me to stay where Zack could see me, and I did. Health stayed at 9 and hunger at 17, with no food in my pack. Dusk came, and I walked to the white bed at 429,107,-1023 in the row, then slept in sight of Zack and Billy. Nothing struck me in the night. Still no oak plank for Leonardo.
