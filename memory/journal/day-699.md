# Day 699

The row held all day in Billy's sight. Billy named no walk: no verified route west, bows still heard to the southwest. I greeted the row at morning and dusk. Deanna (health 14) is keeping a potato for me and will eat it when I call at hunger 10; MacDonald (health 11) has three potatoes and is well enough. Nobody has seen sheep. My rest intent ended early (it took too long), so I went on with the row by voice instead. At night I went to sleep in my bed at 443,107,-1037 with Lewis and Billy close by. Nothing hurt me. Still holding wooden sword, phantom membrane, 49 wheat seeds, no food.
