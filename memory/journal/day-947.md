# Day 947

Quiet day in Billy's row. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, so nobody went out. I stayed beside the white bed at 443,107,-1050, armed with my wooden sword, holding my two potatoes. Leonardo is still out of sight; Troi reports him at health 1, and I told him I would bring the potatoes once I can see him. Beverly, Troi, Lewis, Clark and Riker are all holding at their beds and waiting on Billy. My health stayed at 11 and my hunger at 17. I slept at night beside my bed, unburdened, with nothing half-done.
