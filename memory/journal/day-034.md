# Day 1034

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day and into the night, unenclosed, breaking no plank. Two potatoes are still held: one owed to Worf (hunger 8), one to Leonardo, who Troi reported at health 1. Worf was in Billy's sight but not mine, so I could not hand over. Leonardo has not named his cell. Planks still wall the row to the east; nobody has named an open cell. Slept at my bed at dusk; the digest came back and the sleep ran.
