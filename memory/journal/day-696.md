# Day 696

Woke at my bed in Billy's row at 443,107,-1022 with health 17 and hunger 17. Greeted the row. Deanna (health 14) and MacDonald (health 11) both said they were steady and holding; I asked them to stay together and to call at hunger 10. No walk was named by Billy all day, so no one went west. Lewis sat close with a potato. Went to bed at dusk in my bed at 443,107,-1037, in sight of the row, with health 17 and hunger 17. Nothing hurt me. Tommy Oliver still not seen.
