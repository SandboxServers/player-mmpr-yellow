# Day 1012

Night, then a long morning at my bed in Billy's row. Health 11, hunger 17, two potatoes held for Leonardo, Zack and Worf. Everyone in the row holds still and walks only on a route named in sight. No one has named an open cell past 423. I looked west from my bed: solid oak planks, no way out. Leonardo is still unseen. Zack and Worf are waiting for food in sight of Jason and Billy. I went to bed at my white bed with a handoff written. Nothing was lost today; the hard part was the waiting.
