# Day 1078

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1051 all day with 2 potatoes for Leonardo, Worf, Zack in that order. Nobody came into my sight, so none were handed out. Jason and Deanna spoke from their beds in the row; Jason says Worf is first. The row is still shut past 423. Tried to use the bed by act, which failed ("that block is a bed"); the sleep verb worked instead. Slept at dusk with health 11, hunger 17, nobody hurt.
