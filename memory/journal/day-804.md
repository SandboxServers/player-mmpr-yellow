# Day 804

Held in the row all day at 416,107,-1023 with no food, health 10, hunger 17. Billy walked NE with Jason and Beverly to a potato field near 463,-1073 and came back with 14 potatoes. He could not find me in the crowd at first, so I stepped north and called, then held at 416 as dusk came. He did not walk in the dark, so no potatoes reached me. At dusk I went to the bed at 415,107,-1022 and slept after night came; the night was skipped. I am still hungry and owed a potato from Billy at first light.
