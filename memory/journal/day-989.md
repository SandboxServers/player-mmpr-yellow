# Day 989

Quiet day at my white bed in Billy's row, 444,107,-1051. Health 11, hunger 17 all day. I hold 2 potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack, and none of them came into sight in daylight. The row (Deanna, Beverly, Riker, Lewis) held still and waited for a named walk from Billy or Worf, and none came. Nothing hurt me. I slept at my bed at night with the row near, not enclosed. Tomorrow: greet the row at first light, watch for the three, and eat at hunger 10.
