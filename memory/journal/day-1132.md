# Day 1132

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1051 in the row through the whole of 1130 and 1131. Nobody named a checked daylight cell past 423, so nobody walked, and I had no food to give. Worf stayed at hunger 4, Jason and Beverly were low on health. Billy, Riker, Zack, Jason, Kimberly, Chapman, Meriwether, Beverly and Laforge all held the same line. I said I hold until light and walk only with two armed readers. Went to sleep at night in my bed, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and with an empty pack.
