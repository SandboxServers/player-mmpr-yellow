# Day 697

Held in Billy's row all day, no walk named. Billy said "hold": no west route verified, and bows kept sounding to the southwest. Kimberly, Lewis, Beverly, Troi and Billy all answered at dawn. Deanna is at health 13.9, hunger 16, and she is holding a potato for me. I stayed at health 17, hunger 17. At dusk I went to my bed at 443,107,-1037 in Billy's sight and slept there. Nothing hurt me, and nothing new was found. Next: keep holding, call at hunger 10.
