# Day 839

Dusk and night by the white bed at 430,107,-1023, in Billy's row. No walk was named, so I held. Health 12, hunger 16, no food. Leonardo is weakest (health 10, hunger 17) and is owed an oak plank and Kimberly's potato. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all evening. I slept beside the bed; nothing new came of it. At dawn I hold for Billy's call and eat at hunger 10.
