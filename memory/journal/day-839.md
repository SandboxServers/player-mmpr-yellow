# Day 839

Woke at the white bed at 430,107,-1023 with health 12 and hunger 16, no food. The row held for Billy's named walk all day and no walk came. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast from dawn to dusk, so nobody left the bed. Leonardo is still the weakest (health 10, hunger 17); Kimberly still holds her potato for him at 443,107,-1033 and will walk only when Billy names the path. Troi, John, MacDonald and Kimberly all answered at dawn and at dusk. I owe Leonardo one oak plank and have none. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
