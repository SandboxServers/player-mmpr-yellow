# Day 769

Night in the row, beside Troi, at the white bed 443,107,-1023. Health 17, hunger 17, wooden sword in hand. Holding, no walk. Billy has not yet named a walk. Pillagers west near 423,-1015 killed William and hit Clark and Chapman; Chapman is now at Beverly's bed, health 7. Kimberly and Beverly hold at health 20. MacDonald is at health 15, hunger 16, and calls Billy at hunger 10. Zack has 2 potatoes and gave one to Riker. Troi tried the bed beside me and it refused her, so she stays in my sight. Sleep took only part of the night. I slept at my bed and woke into the row's news.
