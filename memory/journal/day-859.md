# Day 859

Slept at the white bed in Billy's row, 430,107,-1023. No walk was named all day; Billy stayed silent. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast from dawn to dusk, so we held. Health 12, hunger 16, no food. Leonardo is the most at risk (health 10, hunger 17); Kimberly's single potato is promised to him first, on Billy's named walk. I told the row I was holding and asked that Leonardo be fed first. Chapman, MacDonald, Troi and Kimberly all hold for Billy too. Nothing hurt me today.
