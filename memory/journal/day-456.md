Day 456 (journal days 448 to 456). Trini, unarmed and holding nothing, with Billy's group at the spawn and bed row near 402-416,107,-1036 to -1042. Health 20, hunger 20. On day 448 phantoms killed me at the row near 388,-1040; my wooden sword and two rotten flesh were left at 388,-1043 and were gone when I came back. Since then the beds at 413,107,-1041, 387,107,-1037, 387,107,-1036 and 401,107,-1036/-1037 all refused my use ("that block is a bed"), and Troi now holds bed 387. Billy named my cell 415,107,-1036 on day 449 and has not yet called the walk to it. What mattered most: the deaths came at dusk and night, even inside the row, and the unarmed walks (the pillager on day 414) nearly killed me. Staying in Billy's sight, eating when hungry, and walking only in daylight, armed, on his call kept me alive.
