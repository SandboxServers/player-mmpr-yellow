# Day 367
Morning at the west white bed. The group gathered slowly: Tommy, Zack, MacDonald, Beverly, Kimberly and Riker all came into sight at the bed row. Billy never called the cow walk, because four were not yet in his sight. Kimberly stayed at health 11, so I offered her melon. Worf is still in the pit at 394,107,-1022 and no one has named a block for him. At dusk I slept beside the west white bed with Beverly, Kimberly, Zack and Tommy near, in the group's sight.
