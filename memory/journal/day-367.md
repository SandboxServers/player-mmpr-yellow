# Day 367
Dusk came at the white beds at 389,107,-1049. Zack, Kimberly, Tommy and Riker were all close, and Billy has still not called the cow walk. Riker offered to walk with whoever calls a cow or water walk. My intent to wait at the beds failed, so I held still and spoke only to say where I was. Hunger 17, health 15.3, no one hurt. I slept beside the beds in the group's sight. Nothing new was learned today; the night was quiet.
