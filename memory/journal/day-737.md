# Day 737

Held at the row all day with Billy, Jason, Zack, Lewis, Worf, MacDonald, Wesley, Beverly, Troi and Chapman. No walk: nobody has a verified route west past x=370, so Billy kept us at our beds in sight. Hunger stayed at 17 all day, above the call at 10, so no food was needed. Health 17 for me. I told Billy and the row that I had no route either. Troi's health is lower (about 14). Jason's sleep failed in daylight; Billy explained sleep works only at night in a bed. I slept in my bed at 430,107,-1051 at dusk, in sight of the row. Still owed: one oak plank for Leonardo. Tommy Oliver not seen.
