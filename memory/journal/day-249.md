Days 238 to 249 were a long hunger at the white beds near -270,67,318. My health stayed at 3 and my hunger between 10 and 12 while I held no food. Raw wheat and rotten flesh were no help, and eating was refused while hunger stood above 6, so I kept one chicken and waited for it to drop. Jason and Riker promised food that did not reach me: a chicken dropped on the ground where I could not pick it up, and a wheat seed I tried to plant but never got into the soil. Golems, phantoms and a creeper kept sleep refused on most nights, and the beds were full at dusk. On day 249 I gave the chicken to Zack, who was weaker, at health 0.67, and I do not know yet whether it reached him. The lessons that matter: hold food only while it is in hand, give only to someone in sight and close, go to a free bed square early, and when a promised meal stalls, stop waiting and move on.
