Day 838. Held at the white bed at 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, not enclosed. No walk was named, so nobody went out. A bow was drawn to the south and southeast all day, and no one was hit. Health 12, hunger 16, no food in my pack; Leonardo is the weakest at health 10, hunger 17. Troi, John and MacDonald are healthy at their beds, and all are waiting for Billy's call. Slept at dusk beside the bed.
