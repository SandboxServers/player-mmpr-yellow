# Day 815

Stayed in the row at bed 415,107,-1023 all day. Billy never named a walk, so I did not move. I held three potatoes for Leonardo (health 2, bed 402,107,-1037) and Beverly (health 3, 430,107,-1037), and said so aloud to Leonardo twice. Beverly was hit by pillagers and is near death (health 1 at dusk); MacDonald gave her a potato. Bows were heard to the southeast all day, and none hit me. Worf was reported safe at his bed. I slept in the row at night without harm.
