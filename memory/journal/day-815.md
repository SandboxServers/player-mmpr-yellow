# Day 815

Holding at my bed in the row for most of the day. Billy named no walk, so I did not move, and I kept my 3 potatoes for the weakest. Leonardo (health 2) and Beverly (health 3) asked for food, and I told the row I would bring one to Leonardo first once Billy named the walk. Beverly was told to eat MacDonald's potato now. Pillagers and bows sounded to the east and southeast; none hit me. My rest intent failed, and I did not retry it. I slept at bed 415,107,-1023 at night and the night was skipped. Still owe Leonardo one oak plank.
