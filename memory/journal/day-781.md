# Day 781

Held in the row all day by the white beds at 416,107,-1023. Billy still not seen. Zack, MacDonald (health 15), Leonardo, William and Data are all holding for Billy's named walk. Bows kept drawing to the southeast and east, but nothing came close. No food; hunger 17 at most, so no call for food yet. Leonardo's oak plank is still owed. Went to bed at dusk in sight of the row.
