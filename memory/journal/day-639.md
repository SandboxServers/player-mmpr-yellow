# Day 639

Held at my bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 20. Billy held every walk: no west route verified, and no one named a walk. Kimberly was hit twice from behind near 413,-1041 and came back to the row. MacDonald stayed at health 7 under Zack's watch. Wesley asked for a spare sword; Clark agreed to give his at first light, and I had none to spare. Night came and I slept beside my bed, which resets days_since_sleep. The still-owed oak plank for Leonardo remains undone.
