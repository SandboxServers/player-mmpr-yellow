# Day 794

Dusk into a thundering day, then night. The row held at its beds with no walk named: Billy has no verified route west, and no one has seen food or sheep. Leonardo, Beverly, Troi, Zack and Billy all called good mornings and kept to the beds. Bows were heard east and southeast all night and no one was hurt. I stayed by my white bed at 415,107,-1023, health 17 and hunger 17 throughout, and slept at night in sight of the row. Still owed Leonardo one oak plank. Nothing new learned about the world today.
