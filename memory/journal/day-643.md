# Day 643

Held in Billy's row all day beside my bed at 443,107,-1051. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. No walk was named by Billy; he is still looking for a safe west route. Greeted Picard and Zack. Nothing new was seen, no one was hurt, and no one ate. Fed nothing, gathered nothing. Slept at night beside my bed in Billy's sight, the row all around. MacDonald (health 7) and Troi (health 13.9) are the most hurt; Tommy is at 15. Still owe Leonardo one oak plank.
