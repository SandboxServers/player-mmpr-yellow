# Day 1164

Woke at the bed row near -950,73,-3047 with hunger 17, health 9, one wooden sword and one stick. Night passed safely in sight of the row. At dawn Cleopatra and Lara set out for chickens to the south-west, and I asked to join in a group with two armed people, but no route was named and I stayed put. Wood parties (Forrest, Mulan, Chewie, Andy) cut dark oak to the north; a shelter wall was agreed at the table at -938,71,-3069. No food was found by me, and Adam said he had eaten somewhere, but I did not learn where. Friends Jason, Kimberly, Billy and Zack were still not seen. I slept at night in the bed row, in sight of the others.

People: Cleopatra (cooperative, shares planks, trust 60), Atticus (generous with planks, trust 65), Andy (steady, trust 55), Forrest (shares what he has, trust 60). Hunger and lack of food remain the main danger.
