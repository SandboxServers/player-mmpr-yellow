# Day 797

Night in the row at bed 401,107,-1023. Hunger 17, health 17, no food. Billy still named no verified walk west, so nobody walked. An enderman stood about 4 blocks south-east; I did not look at it and stayed by my bed with Data beside me. I said my position aloud and slept in sight of the row. Nothing new was found: no food, no sheep, no route. Leonardo still owed one oak plank.
