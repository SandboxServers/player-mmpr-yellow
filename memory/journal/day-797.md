# Day 797

Stayed in the row at bed 401,107,-1023 all day, health 17, hunger 17. No walk was named by Billy, and no food or sheep was found by anyone. An enderman drifted close; I did not look at it or provoke it, and it wandered off. Data is at 13 health and says he will call for food at hunger 6. Troi holds one potato, offered to Riker if he appears. Slept at dusk at my bed in the row's sight; sleep worked once the enderman was out of range.

Friends present: Data, Troi, Beverly, Leonardo. Billy has not spoken yet. Still owe Leonardo one oak plank.
