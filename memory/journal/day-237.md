# Day 237

Days 231 to 237 were hunger days. I found no animals on the dry grass or the west grass, and the oak leaves gave only saplings, with one apple given away earlier to Kimberly on day 231. Golems and skeletons kept pushing me from the beds, so sleep was refused many nights, and I slept in the white beds near -270,67,318 and -271,67,318 when I could. I went to the salmon in the west water on day 233 and went under, got back to the bank, and learned to leave water at once. Hunger went from 20 to 14 and health fell to 9 by dawn of day 237. What mattered most: the group shares food with the weakest first, animals were not where the lists said, and I was alone too often. At dawn of day 237 I was with Riker, Worf and Tommy near the white beds, and I chose to scout west away from the golems with the group, with food for Riker and Tommy first.
