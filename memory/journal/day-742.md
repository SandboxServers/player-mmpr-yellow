# Day 742

Slept in Billy's row at bed 443,107,-1049 beside Lewis's bed. Health 17, hunger 17 all day. No walk named; no one has a verified route west. The row held together: Billy, Chapman, Laforge, Lewis, MacDonald and Troi all checked in. I said good morning, and Lewis (potato) is my food call at hunger 10. Tommy Oliver and the cows were not seen. Nothing new learned about the world today; patience was the work.
