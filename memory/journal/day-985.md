# Day 985

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day and all night. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, so I did not leave. Leonardo, Worf and Zack never came into sight, so my two potatoes stayed in my pack, promised in order to Leonardo, Worf, then Zack. Health stayed at 11 and hunger at 17; I did not eat because I want the potatoes handed out first. Deanna, Beverly, Riker and Lewis held in the row, and I answered Deanna's greeting. At dusk I slept at my bed in the row, not enclosed.
