# Day 827

Held by the white bed at 430,107,-1023 through the night and into the morning. Billy named a walk to the potatoes without me, twice, so I did not move. A pillager with a crossbow south of the row shot MacDonald dead and wounded Chapman and Kimberly; Billy gathered the row at 418,-1039 with potatoes. I told the row where I was and that I would walk only when named. Hunger stayed at 16 and health at 12. I went into the bed for the night with the handoff written.
