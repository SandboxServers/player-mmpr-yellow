# Day 689

Held in Billy's row all day beside my bed at 443,107,-1022. No walk was named, so nobody left the row. Kimberly, Zack, Deanna, Billy and Picard all checked in and all held. Bows were drawn to the southwest many times, and we stayed in sight of each other. Billy says no safe route west is verified, and Tommy Oliver has not been seen by anyone since the sword. Zack and Kimberly both offered potatoes when I call at hunger 10. Deanna's health is down to 13.9, so I will check on her at first light. Billy moved to the bed at 443,107,-1036 for the night, and I slept at my own bed.
