# Day 828

A long night and morning holding beside the white bed at 430,107,-1023. The pillager stayed to the south and west, shooting at anyone who walked. Billy died once and woke at spawn, then named Troi and Jason as escorts to the 21 potatoes at 409,107,-1054. I was not named, and I did not walk. Billy later told the east beds to hold and not walk west. Troi was shot down to health 2 and then moved east. I ate nothing; hunger stayed at 16 and health at 12. I slept in the bed at dusk. Still owed one oak plank to Leonardo.
