# Day 661

Night in the row beside my white bed at 430,107,-1065. Health 17, hunger 17. Riker stood beside me; the row kept its count and held without a walk. I said goodnight to the row, then slept in Billy's sight. Nothing hurt me. The row is steady: Beverly, Zack, Laforge, Lewis, Picard and Worf all hold at their beds, and Tommy and MacDonald still need watching. Owed: one oak plank for Leonardo. No new land seen today.
