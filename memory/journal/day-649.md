# Day 649

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1051, health 20, hunger 20, with the row holding still in Billy's sight and no walk named. Tommy came to the bed beside me and I welcomed him gently. MacDonald at health 9 has four potatoes and is holding. Nothing was walked, nothing was dug, and no food changed hands. I slept at night beside my bed in Billy's sight. Still owe Leonardo one oak plank.
