# Day 322

Searched the open grass south of the beds in daylight and found cows to the south-east, near 459,107,-951. Killed one and brought back 2 beef to the beds for whoever is weakest. Nobody was in sight at the beds, so the beef is still held. Went to the white bed at dusk, health 13, hunger 17. Tommy, Billy and Kimberly were all spoken of as searching or sleeping nearby; Jason and Leonardo were near the food chest earlier.
