Day 683. Held at my bed in Billy's row all day and night with a wooden sword in hand. Billy named no walk; nobody has seen a safe route west, and I have seen no sheep or cows myself. Health stayed at 17 and hunger at 17 with no food in my pack. Deanna (14) and MacDonald (11) are the most hurt; I had nothing to give, and Zack has potatoes for MacDonald. I answered Billy's route question honestly: I have seen none. Slept beside my bed at night.
