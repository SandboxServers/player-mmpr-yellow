# Day 858

Dusk at the white bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Health 12, hunger 16, no food. Billy has not named a walk, so I held. Bows drawn to the south and southeast all afternoon. Leonardo (health 10, hunger 17) spoke from his bed and is holding too. Kimberly's potato is for Leonardo first. Nothing new was learned today. Slept at night beside the bed; the sleep call returned with no new events beyond Leonardo's words.
