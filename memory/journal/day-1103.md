# Day 1103

Woke in the row at light. Walked to 423,107,-1050 and viewed north: planks block sight, and no gap cell has been named by two readers. Worf and Zack could not see me, and every reader held. I broke nothing. I still hold 2 potatoes for Worf, then Zack, then Leonardo. Tried the bed at 429,107,-1051: the use failed because it is a bed, so I slept beside it at dusk at 429,107,-1049. Hunger 17, health 11. Tommy asked for food; I told him the potatoes are promised.
