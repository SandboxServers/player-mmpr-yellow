# Day 650

Held in Billy's row all day beside my bed at 443,107,-1065. No walk was named, so I did not leave. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Zack, Jason, and most of the row confirmed they were holding for Billy's named call. MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) and Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) are the most hurt, and both hold potatoes. I hold no food, so I had nothing to give. Worf asked after sheep; none have been seen, and cows were last reported far east near 339,104,-979. Slept at dusk beside my bed.
