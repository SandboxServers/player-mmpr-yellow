# Day 650

Held in Billy's row all day, beside the white beds at 443-444,107,-1051 to -1065. Health stayed at 20 and hunger at 20, so no food was needed. Billy named no walk, so nobody went out. Zack, Jason, Lewis, Laforge, Tommy, Beverly and MacDonald each spoke in the morning and at dusk. MacDonald is still at health 9 and was eating a potato, which is the one thing to watch. Tommy is quiet and at 15 health, holding two potatoes, and he has come close to the row. At dusk I slept beside the bed at 443,107,-1065. Nothing attacked in the night.
