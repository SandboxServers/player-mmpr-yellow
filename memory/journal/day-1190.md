# Day 1190

Woke at the bed row near -948,73,-3047 with hunger 17 and health 18.6, holding 6 melon slices for the weakest. The crowd at the row stayed within 2 blocks all day, so no give was made. Zack, Billy and Tommy Oliver are at the row; I joined them and proposed a group search for Jason and Kimberly, but nobody has seen either of them. Billy still owes Tommy 2 sticks and waits for a clear moment. Bed use failed on a bed cell again, so I slept in the group's sight; the night passed with the group.
