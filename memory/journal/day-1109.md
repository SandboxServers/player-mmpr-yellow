# Day 1109

Light came and I walked to 423 with the two potatoes for Worf and Zack. The planks past 423 still block sight. Worf and Zack each see only planks from 415 and 416, and nobody named an open cell, so I broke nothing and handed nothing over. Riker asked for potatoes at a cell near 463-470, which I do not read as open. I went to my bed at 429,107,-1051 for the night, holding the two potatoes. Health 11, hunger 17.
