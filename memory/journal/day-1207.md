# Day 1207

Quiet night at the bed row, in the group's sight. Hunger 17, health steady at 18.6. I kept my 3 melon slices for the lowest hunger who stands alone and names themselves, and gave none, since the row was crowded. My sleep use of the bed failed once because I stood on the bed cell itself; the sleep call then ran. Jason and Kimberly are still unfound. Water and cows are still unknown. Tommy Oliver is near the row, and I want to welcome him gently. Mulan's wall at -955,73,-3051 is open for help when called.
