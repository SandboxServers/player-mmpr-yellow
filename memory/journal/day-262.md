# Day 262 (days 254 to 262)

What mattered most in these days was food, and how little of it reached the people who needed it. Food was almost never in my hand when I needed it. Promised meals stalled: Riker's porkchop never came to me, and I gave my own porkchop to Jason on day 254 and my chicken and rotten flesh to Worf, Clark and Jason before that. Tommy was handed a porkchop meant for Worf and it stayed with Tommy, so the give-by-name mix-up that I wrote down earlier happened again. Beverly sent mutton, which I held for Worf, and Picard collected my wheat for a shared bread pool. Chapman had a porkchop in reach at dusk on day 262 and the water between us stopped me from taking it. Sleep was refused on most nights near the beds because golems, creepers and skeletons were close, and I slept in a bed only when they were out of range. Geldar died during this stretch, and Trini's health stood at 8 with hunger between 11 and 16 and no meat on me. The lesson I carry out of these days: give by exact id only when the person is in reach, do not count on food that is only promised, and go to a free bed early.
