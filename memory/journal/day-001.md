# Day 001

The day was hunger and the dark. I woke at spawn more than once with nothing: first I burned after a zombie caught me bare-handed, and later I drowned in deep water at the north-west lake while chasing a salmon at low health; my things lie at -275,60,182 and I have not gone back. I found the group (Trini, Jason, Zack, Billy, Tommy, Kimberly, Picard) on the shore and at the sheep field, and we pooled what we had. Food came from sheep, cows, pigs and chickens, not from the group's stores. Tommy got my porkchops, Jason got the mutton and beef, and most of it went to whoever looked weakest, but a beef meant for Jason went to Tommy by mistake and some food was eaten on the way by my own hunger. Zack died in the night after a zombie and a wolf and respawned. We slept in the beds near the white and yellow beds at -270,67,336 and -272,68,364, and the dirt walls I built were too short to hold. Tonight I am out of food and near the white beds at dusk, waiting to be fed.
