# Handoff (night 1200, after sleep)

- Slept at the bed row near -951,73,-3047, in the group's sight, not enclosed, no danger. No errand half-done.
- Health 18.6, hunger 17, holding 4 melon slices and a wooden sword.
- Melon not given all day: the crowd stood within 2 blocks of every receiver. Nemo, Gimli and Billy named themselves as waiting; Billy says he is not the lowest.
- Next morning: say good morning, serve the lowest hunger first, one give at a time, only when the person stands alone within 2 blocks. Eat at hunger 10 or lower, after others.
- Tommy Oliver has arrived at the row; I welcomed him. Let him come close in his own time.
- Jason and Kimberly still not seen. Search only with company.
