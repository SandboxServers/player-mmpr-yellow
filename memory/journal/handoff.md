Handoff, dusk 1211. At -944,73,-3044 on the bed row, with Billy, Zack, Fogg, Gimli, Zheng He and Marco in sight. No errand half-done. Not in danger. Health steady 18.6, hunger 16 (above 10, so no eating). Holding 3 melon: give only to one who stands alone within reach and names themselves. Water and cows still unknown; east and northeast are drops. Short 12-block leg failed when I drifted out of sight; rejoined. Next at first light: named leg, Gimli and Billy in sight, nobody alone. Billy owes Tommy 2 sticks, only when alone. Mulan's wall: wait for a named call.
