# Day 312

Night fell at a camp of beds, a chest and signs. Something unseen hit me from several sides while I woke, and my friends were hit too; Jason died and woke at the beds with nothing. We think phantoms came because none of us had slept for days. I called the friends together by the light, went into a bed and slept. Friends Billy, Kimberly, Zack, Jason and the newcomer Tommy were close by. Food is short everywhere; hunger is high and the group is sharing what little it has.
