# Day 333

Mostly waiting. Days 331 to 333 passed at the bed row at 415,107,-1035 with the group: Jason, Zack, Wesley, Tommy, Leonardo, Billy, Lewis and Kimberly. The cow walks kept being called off, and no cows were found at 364,-1010 or at 459,-951. Clark still names no free north wall cell, so I placed no planks and kept my 5 planks and 1 raw chicken. The chicken went unused: nobody was hungry enough (hunger 6 or below) to take it, and Leonardo's group had melon. Food is plentiful here. Tommy and Leonardo were the weakest reported, and both were fed by others. Sleep is still owed (9 days since sleep). Tonight I stand by the bed row at night with friends in sight.
