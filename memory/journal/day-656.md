# Day 656

Held in Billy's row all day beside the white bed at 430,107,-1065. Health 17, hunger 17, steady. A phantom struck me at the row earlier (day 654) and I fought it off with the wooden sword. No walk was named by Billy, so nothing was explored. The row sleeps in sight of each other at dusk: Jason, Laforge, Chapman and Tommy all counted in. Tommy holds two potatoes and is still quiet, close to the row. MacDonald (health 11) has not been heard from today. Next: hold in sight at first light, offer food when hunger reaches 10, and wait for Billy's named call before any walk.
