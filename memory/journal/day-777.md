# Day 777

Held in the row all day at my white bed 415,107,-1023. No walk was named by Billy, and Billy has not been seen. Health 17 and hunger 17 all day, no food, wooden sword in hand. Bows were drawn to the east and southeast, none near. Data reported an enderman three blocks south of 402,107,-1022; I did not go there. I said good morning and good night in the row, reminded Leonardo of the plank I owe him, and slept in the row in sight of Zack, MacDonald and Beverly. Nothing new learned about the west route or the chest at 447,107,-1023.
