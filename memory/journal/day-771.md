# Day 771

Dusk at the row, by my white bed at 443,107,-1023, beside Troi. Holding in sight of Billy and the others, no walk named. Bows heard far to the southwest all night. Troi tried her bed beside me and got in. Kimberly, Beverly, Zack, MacDonald and John all held their beds. Health 17, hunger 17 when I slept. Nothing walked, nothing was lost. Still no verified west route, no sheep, no Tommy Oliver. Owed Leonardo one oak plank.
