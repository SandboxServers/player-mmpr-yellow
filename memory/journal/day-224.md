# Day 224

Days 213 to 224 were a long hunger with the group, and it mattered most that the food ran short and the water and the dark kept taking our routes. I was at 1 health for a night after a bed would not take me, and I survived because I stayed still and the others found a pig at -171,74,201. Several things failed: bed squares that were occupied refused me silently, a planks shelter did not place, and the mutton I held for Zack ended up eaten by my own hunger reflex when skeleton arrows took me to 3 health. Things that worked were simple: eat when hungry, give food by exact id when the person is in sight, walk the known dry route east, and call every animal sighted. The pig carrier was killed by an unseen attacker near -241,268, so the group must not go out alone. By the morning of day 224 I was at full health and hunger, but I had lost my sword, held no food, and was scouting south-west on grass with Billy and Jason near -285,66,339, with no animals found yet.
