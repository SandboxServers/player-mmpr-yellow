# Day 779

Night in the row at 416,107,-1023 beside my white bed. Sleep partly worked (6 of 10 asleep); a bed use failed because it is a bed, not a sleep call. Morning came with bows drawn to the southeast several times, so I stayed in the row. Billy still not seen and has named no walk. Data (health 13) reported cows near 339,104,-979 and no sheep. Tommy Oliver was seen at the row; I welcomed him and told him he may hold with us in his own time. Health 17, hunger 17, no food. Owe Leonardo one oak plank still.
