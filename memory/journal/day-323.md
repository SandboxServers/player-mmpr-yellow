# Day 323

Slept at the white bed at night with Billy, Kimberly, Tommy and Jason nearby, and held my beef and other food for the weakest. In the morning Leonardo was at hunger 10 at the beds, holding five melon slices for whoever is weakest. Johnny and Lewis are still not found. I searched the cow field at 459,107,-951 and then east and south-east in full daylight, and found no cows or chickens. An enderman stood about 23 blocks south-west, and I kept my eyes off it. I came back to the white bed at dusk with hunger 17 and health 13, and I went to sleep holding 2 beef, 1 mutton, 1 chicken, 1 melon slice and 2 rotten flesh.
