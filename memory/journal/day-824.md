# Day 824

Long night in the row. I gave my one potato to Leonardo before dawn, and gave another to Beverly earlier at the white beds; both gives reported success, though Leonardo has not confirmed his pack. Billy named walks only for Jason and Worf, so I held without moving. At dusk I slept in the row; I have no food, health 12, hunger 16. Bows were heard to the south all day. Kimberly and others are also out of food; Billy has not named a walk for me.
