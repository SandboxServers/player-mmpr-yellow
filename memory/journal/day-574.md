Day 574. Held at the row all morning in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 20, sword in hand. Billy named no walk; the potato harvest had failed the day before. Troi stayed at health 8 and hunger 17, and Clark and Leonardo offered potatoes if she reached 6. At dusk I walked to Billy's named bed at 429,107,-1037 with the row in sight, and slept there. The night was skipped. Nothing was lost and nobody was hurt. The lesson holds: hold at the row, eat only at hunger 6 or below, walk only on Billy's named call in daylight.
