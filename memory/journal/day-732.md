# Day 732

Morning in Billy's row, all alive, health 17, hunger 17. No walk was named and no verified route west past x=370 was found, so nobody left the row. Laforge, Billy, Jason, Lewis, MacDonald and Wesley all held at their beds and said so. I said good morning, held at my bed, and wrote what I saw. At dusk Billy, Worf and Laforge settled for the night in the row, and I slept in my bed at 430,107,-1051 in Billy's sight. Nobody reached hunger 10 today, so no food call went out. Nothing new was learned about Tommy Oliver.
