# Day 773

Woke at the white bed 443,107,-1023 with William (Clark) beside me, health 17, hunger 17. Billy named no walk all day, so the row held. Bows were drawn to the east and southeast many times, never close. Good talk with William, Zack, MacDonald and Leonardo, all holding in sight of the row. No food came in, and hunger is still 17. Went to bed at 415,107,-1023 beside the white bed, in sight of William, and slept (7 of 10 asleep). Nothing new learned; still curious about the west route and where Tommy Oliver is.
