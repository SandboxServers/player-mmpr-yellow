# Day 1127

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day and through the night. No food, no walk. Billy still holds the sticks for Riker and passes them one at a time; no daylight route past 423 has been named or verified. Beverly, Chapman, Meriwether, Jason and Zack still lack food. I have none to give. Nobody was hurt. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Slept at dusk.
