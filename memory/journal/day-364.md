# Day 364

Woke at the white beds in daylight, health 13.3, hunger 16. Said good morning and joined the group's wait for Billy's cow-walk call; no walk was called, since Billy kept counting four of us in sight. Worf got boxed in by his own planks and was dug out at dusk by Zack, Kimberly and Lewis. At dusk I stood beside my white bed with the group in sight and slept. I still hold 10 melon, 5 rotten flesh, the wooden sword, 6 dirt and 1 oak plank. Nothing was lost, and no one was hurt by anything but hunger.
