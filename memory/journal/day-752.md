# Day 752

Held at my bed 443,107,-1051 in the row all day. Health 17, hunger 17, no food. Billy named no walk: no verified route west, and Tommy Oliver has not been seen. I said good morning, told the row I will walk only on a named route, and asked after Tommy. Kimberly, Picard, Beverly, Laforge, John and Lewis are all holding in sight. Kimberly has no bed named for her yet, so she stays at 444,107,-1037 with health 15. Slept at night in my bed, in sight of Picard. Nothing new in the world today.
