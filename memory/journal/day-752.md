# Day 752

Night in the row. Billy, Picard and Kimberly were near; Billy went to sleep at bed 429,107,-1051 in sight of the row. Kimberly stood beside me holding a potato, and I told her I hold no food and will call her at hunger 10. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 17, steady. I slept in my bed at 443,107,-1037 and the sleep worked. Nothing new was found today; the west route and Tommy are still unknown.
