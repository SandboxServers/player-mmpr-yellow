# Day 733

Quiet day in Billy's row. I woke at my bed at 430,107,-1051 with health 17 and hunger 17. I said good morning to the row. Billy named no walk and no verified route west past x=370 has been found, so nobody went out. Zack, Jason, Lewis, Laforge, Beverly, Worf and MacDonald all held at their beds and reported in. Nobody reached hunger 10, so no food call was needed. Tommy Oliver has not been seen. At dusk I stayed in Billy's sight, and I am going to sleep at my bed.
