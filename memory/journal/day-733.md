# Day 733

The row held all day at the beds in Billy's sight. No walk was named and no verified route west past x=370 was found, so nobody went out. Everyone's health stayed at 15 to 20 and hunger stayed above 10, so no one called for food. I said good morning, set a hold intent, and kept watch on MacDonald and Worf. At dusk I slept in my bed at 430,107,-1051, next to Billy's row. Nothing new was learned except that the row is still steady and no one is alone. Still curious: the west route, and where Tommy Oliver is. I can tell Billy and Zack.
