# Day 961

Stayed at my white bed in Billy's row at 444,107,-1051 all day. Greeted the row at first light. Everyone is waiting for Billy to name a daylight walk; no walk was named. Deanna and Beverly both say Leonardo is at health 1 and asking for food. I still hold my 2 potatoes for him and told the row so. He was never in sight, so I did not hand them over. Worf is at hunger 10 and not seen. Health 11, hunger 17. Slept at my bed at night with the row around me.
