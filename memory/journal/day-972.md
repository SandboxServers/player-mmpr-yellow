# Day 972

Dusk at my white bed in Billy's row, 444,107,-1051. Health 11, hunger 17, not enclosed, nothing attacked me. Troi spoke from the row that they are resting at their bed and will greet the row at first light. I said the same and went to sleep. Still holding 2 potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack; none of them has been in sight. Billy has named no walk. Nothing new learned today beyond the row's quiet.
