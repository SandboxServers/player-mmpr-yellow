# Day 1188

Dusk at the bed row near -947,73,-3050. Billy is here, still without news of Jason or Kimberly. Tommy Oliver is nearby, keeping two blocks clear of the crowd while he waits for the sticks Billy owes him; I welcomed him and will let him come close in his own time. Many others gathered at the row for the night. Hunger 17, health steady at 18.6, six melon slices kept for the weakest. I went to a bed and tried to sleep; the sleep returned the day's digest, and I am still holding my melon and sword.
