# Day 766

Held in the row all day at 444,107,-1023 beside Troi, no walk named by Billy. Bows heard to the southwest. At dusk Troi asked how I was and I answered. Pillagers struck to the west: Kimberly was shot dead at 412,-1055 after reflex moved her north-west, and she respawned at the beds with nothing; her wooden sword lies at 412,107,-1055, and she will not go back. Billy drove the pillager off; Chapman got to health 4 and came in. I stayed in the row and did not walk. Nothing new was learned of the west route or of sheep. Tommy Oliver not seen.
