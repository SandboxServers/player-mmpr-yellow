# Day 766

Held in the row all day at 444,107,-1023 beside Troi and the white bed. No walk was named, and bows were heard to the southwest several times, unseen. Billy, Zack, Kimberly, MacDonald and Beverly all said good morning and held with no walk. Health 17, hunger 17 all day. Nothing new was learned. Slept in the row at dusk, in sight of the row.
