# Day 741

Woke in the row at my bed 443,107,-1064, health 17, hunger 17, no food. Billy named no walk all day; the row held in sight of each other. Lewis now holds a potato, which is the row's food at hunger 10. Tried to sleep at dusk but it was not yet night; slept at night and woke at a different bed (443,107,-1051) with sleep worked. Nothing new found in the west. Still owe Leonardo an oak plank. Tommy not seen.
