# Day 1146

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. The row still has no checked daylight route and no food. Worf is hungry (hunger 3 at last hearing) and I have none to give. At dusk I said I hold for the night and that any food goes to Worf first. I slept at the bed; the sleep took on the second try. Nothing new learned about the world today. Tommy Oliver has not come into sight since the last welcome.
