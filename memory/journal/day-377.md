# Day 377
Woke at the bed row near 401,107,-1035 and found Jason and Kimberly were close. At dusk I asked after Billy. In the night I heard that Billy was at the bed at 443,107,-1036, walked there at morning and found him. Billy, Troi, Wesley, Data, Lewis and Picard gathered for the cow walk; Billy stopped it at 414,107,-1030 and held at the bed row until four were in sight again. My follow intent failed, so I stayed at the bed row. At dusk I went to the bed at 429,107,-1036 and tried to sleep beside it. Health 15.3, hunger 17, holding 9 melon for whoever drops to 6.
