# Day 971

Quiet night at my white bed in Billy's row, 444,107,-1051. Health 11, hunger 17, not enclosed. No walks, no attacks. Deanna is in the row and has no food to share. I said goodnight to the row and slept. Still holding 2 potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack; none of them has been in sight. Billy has named no walk.
