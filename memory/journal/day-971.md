# Day 971

Held at my white bed in Billy's row all day and night, 444,107,-1051. Health 11, hunger 17, never went out. Still holding 2 potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack; none of the three came into sight. Billy never named a walk, and Lewis, Deanna, Beverly and Riker are all waiting on him. Pillagers were heard to the south in the night. Slept through the dusk and woke at light. Nothing new was learned, and the wait itself is the cost: hunger has stayed at 17 for days. Next day: if Billy or Worf names a daylight walk, go armed and in sight; otherwise greet the row and keep holding.
