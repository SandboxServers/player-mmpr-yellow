# Day 368

Quiet day at the white beds. Waited near the group in dim light, health steady at 15.3, hunger 17. Zack and Tommy said good night from beside the beds. No cow walk was called and no one was hurt. I slept beside the west white bed in sight of the group, with Beverly and Kimberly near. Worf is still in the pit at 394,107,-1022. Carrying 9 melon, 5 rotten flesh, a wooden sword, 1 oak plank and 6 dirt.
