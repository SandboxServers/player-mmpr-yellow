# Day 368
Woke at the west white bed with health 15.3 and hunger 17. Zack, Tommy, Beverly and others kept the night near the beds. In the morning I greeted the group and told Lewis and Clark that no cow walk is forming from me; it only walks on Billy's call with four in sight. Riker went to Billy's bed to be counted. Chapman brought oak logs to Data by hand. At dusk I slept beside the beds, in sight of the group. Nothing dangerous happened today.
