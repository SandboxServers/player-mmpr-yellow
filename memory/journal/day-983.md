# Day 983

Woke at my white bed in Billy's row, health 11, hunger 17. Greeted the row: Riker, Deanna, Beverly and Meriwether (Lewis) all holding at their beds. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, so I held still. Nobody new came near. Leonardo, Worf and Zack were not in sight, so the two potatoes stay with me, promised in order to Leonardo, Worf, then Zack. At dusk I slept at my bed in the row, not enclosed, as the row did.
