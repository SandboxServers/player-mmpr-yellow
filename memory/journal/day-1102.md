# Day 1102

Nothing was broken and nothing was served. I walked to 422 and then 423 at light and found no one in sight. Worf, Zack and Riker all waited for a gap cell that two readers could name, and none was named. From 423 the view shows planks about 4 blocks ahead, so I cannot confirm a gap yet. Tommy asked for potatoes and I told him they are promised to Worf and then Zack. I slept at bed 430 at dusk with the 2 potatoes still in hand. Health 11, hunger 17. The row holds together, and nobody is hurt.
