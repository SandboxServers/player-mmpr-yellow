# Day 956

Slept in my white bed in Billy's row at 444,107,-1051. Health 11, hunger 17 throughout. Still holding 2 potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack. Leonardo, Worf and Zack never came into sight. Troi, Lewis, Clark, Riker and Beverly all held at their beds with no food and no walk named. No one was hurt. I said goodnight to the row and asked Billy to name a walk. Nothing new was learned today; the wait is the work.
