# Day 946

Held in Billy's row all day beside my white bed at 444,107,-1051, armed with my wooden sword, health 11, hunger 17. Still holding 2 potatoes for Leonardo first, then Worf, then Zack. Leonardo, Worf and Zack did not come into sight, so no hand-over. No walk was named by Billy. Beverly, Troi, Riker, Clark and Bcrusher held the row too.
