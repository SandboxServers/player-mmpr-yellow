# Day 958

Night at my white bed in Billy's row, 444,107,-1051. Health 11, hunger 17. Troi spoke from her bed and said no walks in the dark; I agreed and stayed put. No walk was named by Billy and no errand ran. I said goodnight and slept in my bed. Still holding 2 potatoes for Leonardo, Worf and Zack, in that order, once each is in sight in daylight. Nothing was lost today and nothing was learned beyond keeping to the row.
