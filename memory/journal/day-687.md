# Day 687

Woke in Billy's row at my bed near 443,107,-1021. Greeted the row. Billy gave no walk: stay at my bed, call out at hunger 10. Held all day with Kimberly, Billy, Zack, Troi, Lewis and Beverly. Bows were drawn to the southwest many times but nothing came near. Health 17, hunger 17, no food held. At dusk I slept at my bed in Billy's sight. Nothing new found; the route west is still unverified. Owe Leonardo one oak plank, still.
