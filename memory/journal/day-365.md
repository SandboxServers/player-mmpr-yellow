# Day 365

Woke at the white beds in daylight, health 13.3, hunger 16. Said good morning and held melon for anyone at 6. Worf was stuck in the gravel west of the beds for much of the day; Beverly, Tommy, Kimberly and Zack all talked him through mining straight up, and he finally walked in toward the beds by dusk. Billy never called the cow walk, so nobody walked out, and I stayed in sight of the group the whole day. Slept beside my white bed at dusk with the group around me. No one was hurt.
