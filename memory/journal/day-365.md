# Day 365

Woke at the white beds at 402,107,-1037, health 13.3, hunger 16. Waited through the day in daylight with the group in sight; no cow walk was called. Worf is still stuck in the pit at 394,107,-1022, waiting for someone to name a block. At dusk I slept beside the white bed; the sleep worked. Billy and Troi slept at the east bed. Nothing was lost today, and no one was hurt in my sight. Still holding 10 melon, 5 rotten flesh, the wooden sword, 1 oak plank and 6 dirt.
