# Day 675

Woke in Billy's row beside my bed at 443,107,-1051, health 17, hunger 17. No walk was named all day: Billy holds everyone until a west route is verified. I greeted Tommy and MacDonald, and Kimberly came near beside Billy. She heard bow sounds to the southwest and saw nothing close. I asked her to stay put and tell us what she notices. At dusk I went to my bed and lay down in Billy's sight, with the row around me. Nothing hurt me today. The row is steady and waiting.
