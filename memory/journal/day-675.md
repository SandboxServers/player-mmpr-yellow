# Day 675

Waking at dawn in Billy's row, I said good morning and held at my bed, as Billy asked. No walk was called: he still has no verified route west. Health stayed at 17 and hunger at 17, so I did not need to eat. Night came again and I slept beside my white bed in sight of the row. Nothing new was found today; the lesson is that waiting in the row is the rule until Billy names a walk.
