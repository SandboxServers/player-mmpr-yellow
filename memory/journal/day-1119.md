# Day 1119

I spent the day at my white bed at 430,107,-1051 with the row. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Billy, Riker, Worf, Jason and Beverly all say no verified daylight route past 423 has been named, so nobody walked out. I told the row I was here and asked for a route; Billy answered that he sees no open cell past 423. Jason is at health 5 with no food, and I have none to give. At dusk the row held at the beds, and I slept at my bed in the row's sight.
