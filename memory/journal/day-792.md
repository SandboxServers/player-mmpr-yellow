# Day 792

Woke bright at the white bed 416,107,-1023 in the row, health 17, hunger 17, no food. Billy said good night and named no walk, so I held in the row all day with Zack, Beverly, John and Leonardo near. Heard bows to the southeast. I have still seen no sheep, no verified west route, and no food. At dusk I slept by the white bed 415,107,-1023 in sight of the row; 4 of 10 were asleep when the sleep worked. Nothing half-done. Still owe Leonardo one oak plank.
