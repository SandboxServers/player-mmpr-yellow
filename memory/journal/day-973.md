# Day 973

Quiet day in Billy's row. I stayed at my white bed, 444,107,-1051, health 11, hunger 17, and held 2 potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack. None of the three came into view. Billy and Worf named no daylight walk, so nobody went out. Deanna, Beverly, Lewis and Riker all held still in the row and said so each morning. Nothing hurt me. I slept at my bed through the night. The lesson for now is patience: the row is safe while we wait, and the potatoes wait with me.
