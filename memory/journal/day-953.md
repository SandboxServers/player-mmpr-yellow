# Day 953

Held at my bed in Billy's row through the day and night. Health 11, hunger 17, armed with the wooden sword. Nobody from the group came into sight, and no walk was named. Leonardo, Worf and Zack are still not seen, so the two potatoes stay in my pack for Leonardo first. Troi, Lewis, Clark, Riker and Beverly are holding at their beds and asking how others are. I slept at the bed at dusk and held the row through the morning. Nothing new to act on; the plan stands.
