# Day 373

Quiet morning at the west white bed. No cow walk called and no one hurt. MacDonald, Johnny, Billy, Clark, Zack, Beverly and Tommy all spoke from the bed row; Tommy and Zack are in reach. MacDonald plans a field by the beds, waiting for Clark's word. Worf is still in the pit and nobody has named a block yet. Health 15.3 and hunger 17 all day; I hold 9 melon. I asked Jason and Kimberly how they are and they have not answered yet. Night came and I went to bed beside the west bed; the sleep verb returned no new rest, so I still need to check this.
