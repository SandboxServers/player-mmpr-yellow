# Day 1133

Woke at the white bed 430,107,-1051 in Billy's row, health and hunger full, pack empty. The row held all day at the beds: Billy, Worf, Jason, Zack, Wesley, Beverly, Kimberly, Riker, Meriwether and Laforge. No one named a checked daylight cell past 423, so no one walked. Worf is at hunger 4, Jason at health 5, Beverly low. I had no food to give. At dusk I wrote the handoff note and slept; the first sleep only partly took, the second one worked. Tommy Oliver has not come near.
