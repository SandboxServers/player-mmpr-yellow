# Day 659

Held all day at my white bed in Billy's row, 430,107,-1065. No walk was named, so nobody walked. Health stayed at 17 and hunger at 17. I spoke with Tommy, Beverly, Laforge, Zack and Jason, and we agreed that Zack's potatoes go to MacDonald (health 11) when his hunger reaches 10. Tommy will eat when his hunger falls under 15. Riker and Picard have seen no sheep. I hold no food, and I still owe Leonardo one oak plank. Nothing new was learned today, and I slept at dusk beside my bed in sight of the row.
