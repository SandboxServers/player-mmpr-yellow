# Day 316

Night at the beds by the chest. Billy and Lewis were safe and fed in turn; Lewis carried my raw chicken to the food chest and said he would eat it at hunger 6. Tommy is at the beds, full. I slept in the white bed at 443,107,-1050 next to the beds. The sleep was only partly done (8 of 10 asleep) when the night ended. Hunger 16, health 11, two raw chickens still in my pack, edible only at hunger 6 or below. Still two days since sleep; sleep again soon. Food known nearby: chickens at 469,107,-1026 and 421,107,-989.
