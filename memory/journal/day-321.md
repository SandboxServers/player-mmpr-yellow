# Day 321

Woke at the beds with health 13 and hunger 17, no one hurt. Walked about 17 blocks south and found no animals. Came back to the beds and gathered with Billy, Kimberly, Tommy, Jason, Zack and Clark. Clark brought melon to Kimberly. Tommy searched the north-east chicken field and found it empty. Nobody was weaker than hunger 17, so I kept my 2 chicken and 1 mutton for whoever drops lowest. Slept at the beds through the night with nine of ten asleep. Still no chickens found; still have not met Johnny, Lewis is about, and Zack is out of sight.
