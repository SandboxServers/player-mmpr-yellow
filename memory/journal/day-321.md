# Day 321

Kimberly came to the beds at health 8 and asked for melon. My hand-overs of melon and mutton kept failing with "not in sight" even when she stood in reach, so I stopped and kept the food. Jason arrived with five melon and fed Kimberly, which was the real help. Billy has not seen Zack since the handoff, and Jason is looking for Johnny. Tommy says chickens wander open grass outside the fence. I slept at the beds at dusk with one melon and one mutton held for whoever drops lowest.
