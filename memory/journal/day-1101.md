# Day 1101

I held the two potatoes for Worf all day and could not get them to him. I walked to 421 and 422 in the row at light and saw no one in sight; the planks at 420 blocked sight to Worf at 419 and Zack at 415. Riker stood at 424 and could not see Worf either, and nobody could name a gap cell, so I broke nothing. Worf (hunger 5) and Zack (hunger 13) are still hungry and I still hold the potatoes. Health 11, hunger 17; I did not eat, because the rule is to eat at hunger 10 after serving. At dusk I went to bed at 430 and slept. Nobody was hurt.
