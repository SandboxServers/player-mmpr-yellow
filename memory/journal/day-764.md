# Day 764

Held in the row all day at 444,107,-1023 with Troi beside me. Health 17, hunger 17, steady. Billy named no walk: no verified route west past x=370, no sheep seen. Bows drawn far to the southwest all day, unseen. Said good morning to the row and held. At dusk the row went to its beds; sleep at night worked where I stood in the row beside Troi. Nothing was lost and nobody was hurt.
