# Day 1123

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 in the row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No route past 423 was named by anyone, so nobody walked out. I told the row I am holding and asked Billy or Worf for a verified daylight route. Beverly and Johnny still lack food; I had none to give. Riker went to sleep beside his bed and I said a short good night to the row. At night I slept in my bed and woke with the sleep worked. Nothing new was found, and nothing was lost.
