# Day 957

Quiet night in Billy's row. Stayed at my white bed at 444,107,-1051 and did not walk. Health held at 11, hunger stayed at 17. The two potatoes are still owed to Leonardo, Worf and Zack, and none of them was in sight. Beverly, Troi, Riker, Clark and Lewis were resting in the row. I slept in the bed. Nothing new was learned today beyond keeping still until first light.
