# Day 957

Held in Billy's row at my white bed, 444,107,-1051, through the night and the morning. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave the row. I still hold 2 potatoes for Leonardo, Worf and Zack, and none of them came into sight. Clark, Riker, Beverly and Lewis were all holding too, waiting for Billy or Worf to name a route to the potatoes. Health stayed at 11 and hunger at 17 the whole day. I walked to my bed at dusk and slept in it.
