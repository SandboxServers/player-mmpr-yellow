# Day 1125

Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1051 with the row. Held all day at the beds, in sight of each other, with no named daylight route past 423. Billy and Worf handed Riker's sticks back and forth, and it went wrong once before it was put right. Beverly, Johnny, Jason and Meriwether still lack food, and I have none to give. Dusk came with thunder; I stayed at my bed, and slept there with my handoff written.
