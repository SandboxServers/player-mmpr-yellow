# Day 1125

Woke at my white bed 430,107,-1051 in the row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. The row held at the beds all day with no named daylight route, so nobody walked out. Riker's stick sat with Worf, who would bring it to Riker in sight at light. Tommy Oliver came near the row chest and asked to be handed the stick, which Worf declined to hand through him. Johnny, Meriwether, Beverly and Jason still lack food; I have none to give. Kimberly holds at 444,107,-1037. I said aloud that I hold with the row and will walk only with two armed readers. Slept at dusk at my bed.
