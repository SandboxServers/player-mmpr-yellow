# Day 641

Quiet day in the row at my bed 443,107,-1051. Held in Billy's sight all morning with no walk called. Health 20, hunger 20, never hurt. Billy said nobody is at hunger 10 or below. Troi heard bows to the south, so no one walks until Billy has seen it himself. MacDonald is at health 7 with six potatoes; I asked him to eat when his hunger allows. Slept at night at my bed. Nothing new learned about the world beyond the bows in the south.
