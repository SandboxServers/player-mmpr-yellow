# Day 1179

The enderman kept coming back to the bed row all day, so sleep was refused until dusk. I stayed still, did not look at it, and held my 7 melon slices for the weakest. Odo (health 13, hunger 17) waited alone near the chests with slices of his own; I never reached him. At dusk the enderman had left, and I slept in a bed at the row in the group's sight, waking on day 1180. Health stayed at 16.6 with hunger 16, which means no natural healing while hunger is low. Lara, Spock, Seven, Zack and Hatshepsut were the ones handing food around; I kept my slices for whoever named themselves weakest.
