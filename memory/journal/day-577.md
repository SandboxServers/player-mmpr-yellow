# Day 577

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight, armed, health 20, hunger 20. Billy handed potatoes to Tommy, and beds were named for Jason, Worf, Riker and others. No walk was named for me and I went out on none. Troi stayed at health 8; I hold no food to give. My sleep failed at dawn because it was day, so I went to my named bed at dusk and slept there in the row's sight. Still owe Leonardo one oak plank, and my sword is still with him.
