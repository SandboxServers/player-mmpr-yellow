# Day 1149

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. No checked daylight route was named, so nobody walked. Worf, Beverly, Zack and Laforge are low on food; I had none to share. Kimberly asked me for potatoes for Beverly; I had none. Jason, Riker, Lewis, Laforge, John and Wesley all held the row too. Tommy Oliver has not come into sight. I slept at the bed at dusk; sleep worked.
