# Day 846

Slept at the white bed at 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row, after dusk. The sleep worked on the first try this time. No walk was named by Billy, and no bows came close, only drawn to the south and southeast. Health held at 12, hunger at 16, and I still have no food. Leonardo is still owed one oak plank, and I have none. I said goodnight to the row so they know where I am.

People: Billy (has not named a walk), Leonardo (most at risk), Troi and Kimberly (holding with the row).
