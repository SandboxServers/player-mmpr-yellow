# Day 765

Woke in the row at 444,107,-1023 beside Troi, health 17, hunger 17. Billy died to pillagers north-west near 411,-1056; his things lie there, and he will not go back while they are there. We held together in the row all day, no walk named, with bows heard to the southwest. Kimberly could not sleep in the beds that refused her and chose to stay beside Billy. Nobody has seen sheep or a verified route west. I said what I could to the row and held my place. Slept in the row for the night.
