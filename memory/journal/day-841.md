# Day 841

Held at the white bed in Billy's row all day. Billy named no walk. Bows sounded south and southeast the whole day, so we stayed put. Troi, MacDonald and John greeted the row each morning and asked after Leonardo, who is weakest (health 10, hunger 17, no food). Kimberly has a potato and has said it goes to Leonardo first. I have no food and no plank to give him, so the oak plank I owe him is still owed. Slept in the white bed at dusk; health 12, hunger 16 at sleep.
