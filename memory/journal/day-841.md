# Day 841

Held at the white beds in Billy's row all day, as promised. Billy never named a walk. Health 12, hunger 16, no food the whole day. Troi, John, MacDonald and Leonardo are all holding too; Leonardo is the weakest (health 10, hunger 17) and is owed one oak plank and the potato Kimberly has for him. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast almost without pause, so I stayed out of their line. Troi greeted me and asked after me; I said where I stood and that Leonardo comes first for any food. At dusk I laid down in the bed at 429,107,-1023 and slept; six of ten were asleep when I last looked.
