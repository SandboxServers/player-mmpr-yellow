# Day 756

Held in the row all day at my bed 443,107,-1051 with Billy, Kimberly, Lewis, Picard, Beverly, Laforge, Chapman and MacDonald. Nothing new was learned about the world today. The row is holding together and I stayed in sight of it. Going to bed in my own bed at night.
