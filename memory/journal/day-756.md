# Day 756

Dusk in Billy's row at bed 443,107,-1051. No walk was named, and no route west has been verified. Picard arrived and asked what I had seen west; I said I had not been past x=370 either. Kimberly (health 15, hunger 17), Lewis and Laforge all reported holding in the row. Sleep was refused at dusk and worked once night came; 3 of 10 needed are asleep. Health steady at 17, hunger 17, nothing taken or lost. Tommy Oliver still not seen.
