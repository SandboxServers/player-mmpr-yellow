# Day 836

Held at my bed at 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and night. No walk was named, so I did not leave. Health stayed at 12 and hunger at 16 with no food in my pack. Leonardo, at health 10 with no food, is the one I worry for most; the potato is held by Kimberly for whoever is weakest once Billy calls the path. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, and none came close. Troi, Kimberly, Chapman and MacDonald all checked in and are holding too. I slept at dusk beside the bed; the night passed without harm.
