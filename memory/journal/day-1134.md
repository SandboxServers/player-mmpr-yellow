# Day 1134

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No one has named a checked daylight cell past 423, so nobody walked. Nobody in the row has food; Worf is at hunger 4 and Beverly at health 9. Kimberly and Meriwether hold at the spawn beds. Billy said the bed at 415,107,-1050 refuses sleep, and Wcrusher took the bed at 415,107,-1051. I slept at my bed at night with the row in sight, and no one was hurt.
