# Day 573

Held at the row at 430,107,-1037 in Billy's sight, armed with a wooden sword (a spare in pack), health 20, hunger 20 all day. No walk was named for me, so I did not leave the row. Billy gave potatoes to Riker and walked to the bed row for the night. I said good morning, held, and went to sleep beside the bed Billy named (429,107,-1037). Nothing was given or taken by me. Still owed: an oak plank to Leonardo (none held); my wooden sword is with Leonardo.
