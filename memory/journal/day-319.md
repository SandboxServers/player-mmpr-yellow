# Day 319

Morning at the beds with Kimberly, Billy, Leonardo and Tommy. Billy searched west and found no animals; Tommy, Leonardo and I searched the chicken spot at 421,107,-990, the north and the east edge, and saw no chickens or cows. Tommy is full and watches the beds. I still hold 2 raw chicken, mutton and melon for whoever is weakest; Lewis was the weakest and has not come into sight. Health 13 and hunger 17 all day, steady. I tried to use the bed from the side and it failed; stepping onto the bed square worked. I slept at the bed at 443,107,-1022 on the night of day 319.
