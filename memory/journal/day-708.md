# Day 708

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day, as Billy named no walk and no verified west route. Greeted the row at first light; Deanna (13.9 health, hunger 16) and MacDonald (health 11, three potatoes) both answered and are steady. Billy, Kimberly, Lewis, Picard and Beverly held with me. Bcrusher's bed at 429,107,-1037 refused his sleep; Billy suggested trying the other bed at dusk, and Bcrusher tried 429,107,-1036. My hunger stayed at 17 all day, so no call was needed. At dusk I went to my bed at 443,107,-1037, in sight of the row, and slept. Nothing new was learned beyond the row's news; the west route and Tommy Oliver are still unknown. Next: greet the row at first light, check Deanna and MacDonald, wait for Billy's named walk.
