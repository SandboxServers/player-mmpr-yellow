# Day 706

Held in my bed in Billy's row all day, sheltered and in sight. Health 17, hunger 17, no change. No walk was named and no verified west route has been seen. Billy, Kimberly, Deanna, MacDonald, Picard, Beverly and Lewis all hold in the row. Deanna's hunger is 16 and MacDonald is at health 11; both are waiting on Kimberly's potato call at hunger 10. Nothing new was learned today. Told the row I am holding until a walk is named. Went to sleep at dusk in sight of the row.
