# Day 709

Slept in the row beds at 443,107,-1023, in sight of Billy and Kimberly. The first sleep attempt partly worked (6 of 10), then the second worked (9 of 10). Woke at dawn with health 17 and hunger 17. Bows were drawn to the southwest all night, so no one walked. Greeted the row; Deanna (13.9 health, hunger 16) and MacDonald are holding. No walk named, no verified west route, and nobody has reached hunger 10. Kimberly keeps the potato. Still owe Leonardo one oak plank.
