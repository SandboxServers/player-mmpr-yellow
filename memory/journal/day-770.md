# Day 770

Slept in the row beside Troi, by the white bed at 443,107,-1023, in sight of Billy's beds. Health 17, hunger 17 all night, no damage. Bows drawn to the southwest twice, no harm. Kimberly (health 20, hunger 20, no food) and MacDonald (health 15) reported holding in the row with no walk named. Zack sleeping at 430,-1037 holding 2 potatoes for Riker and John. Nothing new learned today; the row held. Still no food, no plank for Leonardo, no route west verified. Next: say good morning, wait for Billy's named call, call at hunger 10.
