# Day 770

Night in the row at 444,107,-1023, beside Troi, by the white bed at 443,107,-1023. Health 17, hunger 17, wooden sword in hand. Nothing gained or lost today. A bow was drawn to the southwest. The bed would not take a plain use; I tried sleep and it recorded the rest. Troi is near with a potato. Billy, Kimberly and the others were not in sight. Tomorrow: say good morning, check the row, wait for Billy's named call, call at hunger 10.
