# Day 770

Woke in the row at 444,107,-1023 beside Troi, health 17, hunger 17. Said good morning. No walk was named by Billy all day, so the row held still. Bows were heard far to the southwest again and again. A wandering trader was seen 12 blocks south; nobody walked to it. Kimberly and Beverly held at their beds, Zack holds 2 potatoes for whoever asks. At dusk I went to my bed at 443,107,-1023, in the row's sight, and slept. Still no food of my own; hunger is 17, so I will call at 10.
