# Day 962

Quiet day in Billy's row. Nobody named a walk. Leonardo, Worf and Zack were not seen, so the 2 potatoes are still with me, promised to them in that order. Beverly, Riker and Troi went to their beds at dusk; Troi and Beverly are holding food. I slept at my white bed at 444,107,-1051, unenclosed, health 11, hunger 17.
