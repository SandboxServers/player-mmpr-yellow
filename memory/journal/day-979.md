# Day 979

Quiet day at my white bed in Billy's row, 444,107,-1051. Health 11, hunger 17 all day, holding 2 potatoes. Leonardo spoke from out of sight and Deanna asked me to hand him the potatoes, but he never came into my view, so they stayed in my pack. Lewis, Beverly, Riker and Deanna are holding in the row and waiting for a walk named by Billy or Worf; none was named. I did not walk. Nothing new was learned beyond the row's voices. I slept at dusk into night at my bed.
