Day 974. Woke at my white bed in Billy's row, 444,107,-1051, health 11, hunger 17. Greeted the row and asked who had seen Leonardo, Worf or Zack. None came into sight, so I still hold the 2 potatoes for Leonardo first. Riker (William), Deanna, Lewis (Meriwether), and Beverly all answered from their beds and are holding for a named daylight walk from Worf or Billy. Nothing new was learned and no walk was named. At dusk I slept in my bed. No harm came to me.
