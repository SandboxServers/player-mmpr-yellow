# Day 691

Quiet day in the row at 444,107,-1023. No walk was named by Billy, so nobody went west. Health stayed at 17 and hunger at 17 all day, so no food was needed. Everyone reported steady: Deanna at 13.9 to 14, Kimberly at 15, the rest at 19 to 20. Heard bows drawn to the southwest all day, so the row stayed put. At dusk I went to bed beside my bed in Billy's sight.
