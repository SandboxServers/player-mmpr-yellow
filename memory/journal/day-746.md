# Day 746

Woke in my bed in Billy's row at 443,107,-1051, health 17, hunger 17. Nobody named a walk all day: Billy, Laforge, Beverly, John, Lewis all held at their beds, and no verified west route has been found. I held still, said good morning, and kept watch on the row. No food, no damage, no new people. At night I tried to use my bed, which failed (it is a bed, not a use target from where I stood), then slept, and nine of ten needed were asleep. Nothing new learned; the row is steady and waiting on Billy.
