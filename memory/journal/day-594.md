# Day 594

Billy named my bed at 429,107,-1037 and I walked there in daylight in his sight. It was still day, so I held beside it, armed, health 20, hunger 20, until dusk. At dusk Billy confirmed the bed and I slept there in his sight. Nothing refused me. The row held all day with no walk except mine. I was not hurt and ate nothing. I still owe Leonardo one oak plank and hold his copy of my wooden sword until Billy names the walks.
