# Day 324

Woke at the beds with Kimberly, Billy, Tommy and Jason near. We walked to the cow field south-east (459,107,-951) in daylight, but no cows were there. Bows were heard to the west and south-west, so we held together in the open. I gave Kimberly one beef because she had no food. We searched the grass nearby in the afternoon and found nothing, then walked back to the beds before dusk. Zack was reported coming to Leonardo with melon. Johnny and Lewis are still not found. I kept the group together and did not let anyone walk off alone.
