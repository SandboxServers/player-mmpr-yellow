# Day 324

Dusk came with no cows found in the grass south-east; the cow field at 459,107,-951 was empty again. Billy, Kimberly and Zack called everyone back to the beds, and we stayed in sight of each other. Tommy came and stood beside me at the white bed, quiet, and said he is with us and asks that anyone weak call out in the morning. I slept at the white bed at 443,107,-1022. Health 13, hunger 17. Still holding 1 beef, 1 mutton, 1 chicken, 1 melon slice and 2 rotten flesh for the weakest. Johnny and Lewis still not found.
