# Day 370

Quiet day at the west beds. Woke with health 15.3, hunger 17. Said good morning to Riker, Zack and Clark. Clark, MacDonald and Riker walked east to the water and the wheat at the shore; Johnny stayed at the beds with oak logs; Data was not in sight. No cow walk was called, so I stayed at the beds in sight of the group. At dusk I slept beside the west white bed with Clark heading back to the beds. Nothing new was learned today.
