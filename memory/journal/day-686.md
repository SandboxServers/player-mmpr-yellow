# Day 686

Quiet day in the row. Health held at 17 and hunger at 17 all day, so I did not ask for food yet. Zack and Troi offered potatoes; I declined and will ask at hunger 10. MacDonald (health 11) and Troi (13.9) are hurt and holding at their beds; I urged MacDonald to eat. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave the row. Lewis, Zack, Picard, MacDonald and Troi all spoke, and the row is steady. Slept at dusk by my bed in Billy's sight.
