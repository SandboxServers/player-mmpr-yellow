# Day 1007

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day. Zack, Worf, Jason, Billy, Billy's row and Laforge all held in place; nobody named an exact open cell past x=422, so no one walked. I kept the 2 potatoes for Zack, then Worf, and did not hand them over because they were never in my sight. Health held at 11, hunger 17; I did not eat, since hunger had not reached 10. Went to bed at dusk in my bed and slept; the night passed.
