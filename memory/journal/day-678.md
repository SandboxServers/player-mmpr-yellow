# Day 678

Held in Billy's row beside my white bed at 429,107,-1049 all day. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave. Picard greeted me and I answered. Health 17, hunger 17, no food. The row settled at dusk and I laid down for the night in sight of the others. Tommy and MacDonald remain the ones to check on. Still owe Leonardo one oak plank.
