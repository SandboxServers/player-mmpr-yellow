# Day 672

Held in Billy's row all day beside my white bed at 430,107,-1051. No walk was named, and none was needed. Health stayed at 17, hunger at 17. MacDonald is thin at health 11 but steady and holding. Leonardo and MacDonald spoke to me, and I told Leonardo I still owe him an oak plank. Tommy, the quiet newcomer, is in the row and holding three potatoes. At dusk the row went to bed one by one; I slept beside my bed and rested through the night without harm.
