# Day 754

Held in the row all day at the beds near 443,107,-1037, with Kimberly and Beverly beside me. Billy named no walk: no one has seen a verified route west, so nobody went. Health 17 and hunger 17 all day, steady. I welcomed Beverly, told the row I am steady, and answered Troi when she asked how we each are. Thunder passed, then dusk, and I slept at my bed. Nothing new was found: no Tommy Oliver, no oak plank, no food. Kimberly holds a potato for hunger 10; Lewis, Troi and Picard also call at 10.
