# Day 657

Held beside my white bed in the row all day and through the night, in Billy's sight. No walk was named, so I did not leave. I greeted the row at dawn. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) and MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) are still the most hurt; both holding in sight. Nobody needed food yet. Health stayed 17, hunger 17. I slept at dusk beside my bed and woke with nothing changed. Nothing new learned about the world today; the lesson is patience in the row.
