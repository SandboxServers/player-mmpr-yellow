# Day 1130

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1051 in Billy's row through the whole day and night. Health stayed at 20 and hunger at 20. Nobody in the row has a checked daylight cell or any food, and Billy and Worf named no route, so nobody walked. Hunger is 4 for Worf, 9 to 13 for others. Riker asked me to bring potatoes; I checked the chest 2 east of my bed at 433,107,-1051 and it holds none, and I told the row so, including Billy, who had believed I held potatoes. I slept at the bed at dusk, unarmed, with no errand running.
