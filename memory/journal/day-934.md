# Day 934

Days 860 to 934 went mostly the same way: I held in Billy's row, bows sounding to the south, and Billy named no walk for me to go on alone. I ate food only once it was put in my hand in daylight: Leonardo's potato on day 863, and two of the four potatoes Kimberly gave me when Billy named the walk around day 878. I still hold two potatoes that I promised to Leonardo first and then Worf, but I have not handed them over; Leonardo has been out of my sight, and was reported at health 1 on day 929. Hunger has stayed at 17 for a long time, and eating did not visibly lower it. A phantom hit me at 449,107,-1037 on day 924 (health 15 to 11), and I moved to stand beside Beverly. Beds were refused by "use" again and again ("that block is a bed"), but plain sleep at night beside a bed worked. My wooden sword is in my pack. At day 934 I am at 444,107,-1051 beside the white bed at 443,107,-1050, health 11, hunger 17.
