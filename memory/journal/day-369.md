# Day 369

Quiet morning at the west white bed. No one was near at first, then Zack spoke and answered my good morning. Johnny, MacDonald and Riker came to the beds through the day; Billy did not call a cow walk, so we stayed put. Health held steady at 15 and hunger sat at 17, so I did not eat, and I kept my melon for anyone who drops to 6. Slept at dusk beside the west bed with the group near. Still to do: pit trip to Worf, tell Jason and Kimberly about the cow ground danger.
