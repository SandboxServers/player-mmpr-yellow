# Day 959

Woke at my white bed in Billy's row. The row held quietly all day: Troi, Beverly, William, Riker and Meriwether all waiting for Billy or Worf to name a daylight walk, and none was named. Leonardo called out for food; Troi had none to give. I greeted the row and kept my 2 potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack, but none of them came into my sight. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 11. At dusk I wrote my handoff and slept at my bed, not enclosed but in the row.
