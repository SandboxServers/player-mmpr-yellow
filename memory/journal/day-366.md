# Day 366

Woke at the white beds, health steady, hunger 17. Good morning said to the group. No cow walk was called all day; Billy kept asking for four in sight, and most of us stood at the beds and east side in daylight. Worf is still stuck in the pit at 394,107,-1022 and no one has named the block to dig. I walked to Zack's view at the west beds in daylight and stayed there. At dusk Zack bedded down and I slept beside him in sight of the group, no walk after dark. Health 15.3, hunger 17 at sleep.
