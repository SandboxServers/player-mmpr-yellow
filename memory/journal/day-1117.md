# Day 1117 (night)

Stayed at the white bed beside 429,107,-1051 with the row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No walk in the dark. Kimberly and Jason are holding awake or sleeping at their beds and waiting for Billy or Worf. No verified daylight route past 423 yet. I said goodnight to the row and slept. At light I will ask Billy or Worf for a verified route, and walk only with an armed reader in sight.
