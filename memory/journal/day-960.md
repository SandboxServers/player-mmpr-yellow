# Day 960
Stayed at my white bed in Billy's row, 444,107,-1051, all day. Health 11, hunger 17, not enclosed. Holding 2 potatoes promised to Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack. Leonardo answered in speech but was never in sight; Worf and Zack not seen. Billy still named no walk, so nobody moved. Deanna has no food. Night came and I slept at the bed with the row around me.
