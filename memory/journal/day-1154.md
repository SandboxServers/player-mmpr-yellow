# Day 1154

Held at my white bed in Billy's row through the night and the dawn. No checked daylight route was ever named by Billy, Worf or Jason, so I did not walk. I have no food and no weapon, and I gave none away. Watson asked me for a potato; I told him the potato cells are not mine to take from. Food moved through the camp: Rocky, Samwise, Mark and Crusoe fed Kirk, Scotty, Worf, Brunel and others. Beverly (health 9) and Worf were the hungriest I knew of. Slept at the bed at dusk with Laforge and McCoy in sight.
