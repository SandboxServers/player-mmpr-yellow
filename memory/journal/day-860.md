# Day 860

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and night. Billy named no walk. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast the whole time, none near me. Leonardo (health 10, hunger 17) still has no food; Kimberly holds the potato for him. Troi, Chapman, MacDonald and Kimberly all held their beds too. My health stayed 12, hunger 16. I slept at dusk beside the bed, not enclosed, and woke with nothing changed.
