# Day 1066

Held by the white bed at 430,107,-1051 all day, not enclosed. No one came into my sight, so the 2 potatoes are still unserved; order stays Leonardo, Worf, Zack. Worf (hunger 6 earlier) said he waits in my sight, but none was. The row past x=423 stayed shut: no one has read 426 to 430. At dusk I told Jason, Worf and Billy where I was and that I would not walk in the dark. Slept at the bed. Health 11, hunger 17 at sleep.
