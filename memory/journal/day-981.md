# Day 981

Morning in Billy's row at my white bed, 444,107,-1051. Troi greeted the row and asked after Leonardo, Zack and me. I answered and held my two potatoes, still owed to Leonardo first. My rest intent failed at once, but the row kept talking: Beverly, Riker, Lewis and Troi all hold at their beds and wait for Billy or Worf to name a daylight walk. No walk came. Dusk fell and I slept at my bed, health 11, hunger 17. Nothing new was seen of Leonardo, Worf or Tommy Oliver.
