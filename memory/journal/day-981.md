# Day 981

Night passed safely at my white bed in Billy's row, 444,107,-1051, not enclosed. Greeted Deanna and Beverly in the morning and told them I hold two potatoes for Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack. Leonardo was reported at bed 416 with 1 health; I said I will carry the potatoes toward him in daylight once Billy or Worf names a walk. No walk was named, so I did not move. Health 11, hunger 17 throughout. Slept at my bed at night. Still holding the potatoes; Leonardo is the next owed.
