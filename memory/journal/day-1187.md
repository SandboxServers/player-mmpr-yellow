# Day 1187

Woke at the bed row near -947,73,-3047 with health 18.6 and hunger 17, holding 6 melon slices for the weakest and a wooden sword. The group gathered there through the night and into the morning, and many are short of food. Odo was fed by Sattler and Florence, and Magellan's log went to Uhura through Fogg. I said aloud that I am looking for Jason, Kimberly and Billy, and Magellan has no news of them. I meant to walk south-west with Indy and did not get there, since the day ran out. Nobody asked me for a give, so I kept the melon. I slept at the bed row in the group's sight. Jason, Kimberly and Billy are still not found.
