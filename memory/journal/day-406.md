# Day 406

Died on night 403 at the bed row (phantoms, health down to 1) after standing on open gravel at 413,107,-1040. My sword, melon and rotten flesh were dropped there and are gone; Kimberly returned one plank. Through days 404 and 405 I held in the group's sight at the bed row with Billy, Troi, Picard, Bcrusher, Kimberly, Lewis, Jason and Tommy, and nobody was struck. Billy, at health 1.5, was fed melon by Kimberly and recovered to 6.6. On day 406 at dusk I slept in a bed at 413,107,-1041 with the group near, and the sleep worked: days_since_sleep reset to 0. I still carry 1 oak plank and have no sword, so I stay out of the cow walk.
