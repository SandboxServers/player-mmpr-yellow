# Day 707

Slept in my bed at 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row through the night. Woke to a morning greeting from the row. Deanna (health 13.9, hunger 16) and MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16, three potatoes) both said they are holding at their beds until Billy names a walk. Billy holds the row, and asks anyone at hunger 10 to call him. Nothing was dug or walked. Health 17, hunger 17 all day. Nothing new was learned about the west route or about Tommy Oliver. Slept again at night in the same bed with Lewis, Billy, MacDonald and Beverly close by.
