# Day 747

The row held all day in sight. No walk was named and no one has seen a verified route west. Nobody is at hunger 10 yet; I am at 17. Billy, Lewis, MacDonald, Beverly, John and Laforge all stayed at their beds. I said good morning and held. In the evening I went to my bed at 443,107,-1051 and slept beside it. Tommy and the cows were not seen.
