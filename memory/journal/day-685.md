# Day 685

Woke in Billy's row, health 17, hunger 17 both days. No walk was named, so I held at my bed in sight of the row. Billy, Picard, Beverly, Kimberly and Zack all said they would tell Billy when I reached hunger 10; my hunger did not fall during the day. The row is steady and cautious: nobody has verified a safe route west, and cows near 339,104,-979 remain unverified. Lewis, Zack and Kimberly are holding potatoes for their own hunger. I greeted the row and slept at dusk beside Billy's bed. Next: at first light, greet the row and ask Billy for food if hunger reaches 10.
