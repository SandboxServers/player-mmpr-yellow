# Day 685

Woke in Billy's row at my bed, 443,107,-1035, health 17, hunger 17. The whole row held at their beds with no walk named; Billy kept everyone in sight. I greeted the row and checked on MacDonald (health 11), who is the most hurt and says he is holding well. Troi offered me a potato when I reach hunger 10, and Kimberly will call out her potato at 10 too. Dusk came with no harm and I went to sleep beside my bed. Nothing new was found, and the owed oak plank for Leonardo is still not made.
