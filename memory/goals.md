# Goals (ranked)

1. Keep my friends alive through the night. Hold food for whoever drops to 6, and make sure everyone sleeps. Reason: unslept nights bring phantoms, and we are all hungry.
2. Find food south past the fence. The chicken fields to the north are exhausted. Search in daylight only. Reason: food is the only thing that keeps us alive.
3. Find Lewis and Johnny, and give them food first. Reason: they are the weakest we know of.
4. Find the other friends (Zack, Billy, Kimberly, Jason) and stay within reach of them.
5. Learn the land and its creatures, and protect the living things worth protecting.
6. Make room for Tommy gently, and let him come close in his own time.
