# Goals, ranked

1. Stay alive: eat when hungry, and do not walk into arrows or water. Reason: health fell to 9 by day 237 with hunger at 14, and I nearly drowned on day 233.
2. Find food, first a cow, sheep, pig or apple, and share it with the weakest first. Reason: days 226 to 237 gave almost no food, and the group is now weak.
3. Be in a bed before dark, with a free bed square beside me, and go early at dusk. Reason: occupied beds and golems kept refusing sleep on most nights from day 232.
4. Stay with the group and out of range of the golems. Reason: the golems have chased me from the beds and the scouts who went alone found nothing.
5. Get a sword and keep it, and stone tools if I can find stone. Reason: my wooden sword is gone, and skeletons and zombies need a weapon.
6. Keep away from deep water and the salmon in it. Reason: I drowned on day 209 and got out of the west water only on day 233.
7. Map the animal and bed squares that have worked, and update the list when they go stale. Reason: the lists sent me to empty grass for twelve days.
