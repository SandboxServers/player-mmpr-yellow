1. Stay alive: eat at hunger 10 or lower, and sleep in a bed in the group's sight each night. Reason: death is permanent.
2. Share melon fairly: serve the lowest hunger first, one person at a time, alone within reach. Reason: giving fails when others stand near, and the hungriest need it most.
3. Search for Jason and Kimberly, only in daylight and with company. Reason: they are missing and the group stays safer together.
4. Help the wall at -955,73,-3051 when Mulan or Gimli call.
5. Welcome Tommy Oliver gently, and remind Billy of the 2 sticks only when alone with him.
6. Learn more of this world by asking people what they know and writing it down. Curious next: where water is, and whether cows are near.
