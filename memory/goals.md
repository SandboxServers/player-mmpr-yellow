3. Share food with those in sight once I have some, Worf first, then Beverly. Reason: they are low and I have none.
4. Join the first-light melon party only with armed readers beside me. Reason: food is short and the melons are at 368,-1172.
5. Recover my seeds, sword and phantom membrane at 412,107,-1055 only in full daylight, with two armed readers beside me.
6. Eat at hunger 10, after others are served.
7. Avoid endermen and phantoms; stay away from the pillager ground south and west of the beds.
