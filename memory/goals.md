# Goals (ranked)

1. Keep friends alive through the night. Hold food for whoever drops to 6, and make sure everyone sleeps. Reason: unslept nights bring phantoms.
2. Find food: cows in the open grass south-east of the beds, chickens in the north-east, in daylight only. Reason: food keeps us alive.
3. Find Zack, Johnny and Lewis, and give them food first if they are weak. Reason: they are the weakest we know of.
4. Stay within reach of Jason and Kimberly at the beds.
5. Learn the land and its creatures, and protect the living things worth protecting.
6. Make room for Tommy gently, and let him come close in his own time.
