# Goals (ranked, as of day 817, second night)

1. Get my one held potato to Leonardo (402,107,-1037, health 4) once Billy names a walk to him with an escort. Reason: weakest, asked for it, and I promised.
2. Stay alive through the night: in a bed in the row before dusk. Reason: phantoms and bows at dusk.
3. Walk only on Billy's named call, in daylight, armed. Reason: his walks have gone to Beverly and Worf, not me; I do not move on my own.
4. Eat at hunger 10; keep the rest for the weakest. Reason: potatoes are scarce.
5. Keep the row together; check on John, Data, MacDonald, Worf. Reason: hurt or hungry.
6. Give Leonardo one oak plank for his sword. Reason: promised, still owed, none in pack yet.
7. Find Worf and Tommy Oliver near the west beds. Reason: not seen since the pit.
8. Do not look at or provoke endermen; do not dig at 394,107,-1025; stay away from 412,-1055.
