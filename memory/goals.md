# Goals (ranked, as of day 1148, after sleep)

1. Stay alive: no walking in the dark, and no going out without two armed readers in sight. Reason: I died three times on night 1112 after walking out alone.
2. Hold at the beds in the row until Billy or Worf names a checked daylight cell. Reason: the row keeps its calls together.
3. Share food with those in sight once I have some, Worf first. Reason: Worf, Beverly and Zack are low; I have none.
4. Recover my seeds, sword and phantom membrane at 412,107,-1055 only in full daylight, with two armed readers beside me.
5. Eat at hunger 10, after others are served.
6. Avoid endermen and phantoms; stay away from 412,-1055, the west past x=408, and the pillager ground south and east of 445.

Tell next: Tommy Oliver, when he comes near in sight, that the row holds at the beds and he is welcome.
Curious next: whether any cell past 423 has a checked daylight route, and whether the potato cells at 464-469,107,-1073..-1075 are ripe in daylight.
