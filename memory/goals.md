# Goals, ranked

1. Stay alive: eat, and do not die again. Two deaths in one day cost me everything. Reason: nothing else matters if I am dead.
2. Get a weapon and a sheltered bed before dark. Reason: zombies and wolves came out at night and I had neither.
3. Keep the group fed, weakest first. Reason: Jason and Tommy went hungry while food was in my hands; the group lives or dies together.
4. Keep within reach of the friends and find the ones still missing. Reason: we are safest in one place; a lone person dies.
5. Learn the land: which animals, lakes and beds are safe. Reason: I learned the hard way where the danger is.
6. Protect the places and living things worth protecting. Reason: this is what I set out to do, but it comes after survival.
