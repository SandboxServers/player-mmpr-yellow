5. Eat at hunger 10, after others are served. Reason: my hunger is 20 now, so this is not urgent.
6. Avoid endermen and phantoms; stay away from 412,-1055, the west past x=408, and the pillager ground south and east of 445. Reason: they have killed or chased several of us.
